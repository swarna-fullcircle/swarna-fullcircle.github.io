About

Full Circle RE Founding Partner Swarna Mohan is committed to providing excellent, personalized service to every client. She zeroes in on her clients’ expectations and uses every tool and resource available to help them, guiding them step-by-step through a systematic process that is proven to deliver.

After obtaining her Texas Accredited Commercial Specialist (TACS) designation in early 2021, she quickly gained experience in residential and commercial land sales and land development, investment purchases and sales — including single- and multi-tenant retail strip centers, flex warehouse, and office condos. She has also represented tenants and landlords for commercial leases.

She is currently building a commercial team by hiring and training new and experienced agents to help with her strong client base. Swarna’s STEM background, coupled with her entrepreneurial spirit, has provided a solid foundation for her successful real estate career.
