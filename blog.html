Williamson & Hays · Data-center policy

Tonight: Georgetown hears Inner Loop Exchange at 6, and Kyle takes up a citywide data-center ban

FOX 7 Austin · KVUE · Texas Data Center Watch · City of Kyle · Nov 2026

Today is the data-center night I’ve been flagging all week. At 6 p.m., Georgetown’s Planning and Zoning Commission holds its public hearing on the Inner Loop Exchange PUD, which would rezone about 28 acres at Northeast Inner Loop and East University Avenue to allow a 68 MW data center of up to 290,000 SF, up from the 5,000 SF the current commercial zoning allows. FOX 7 reports residents from Churchill Farms, Riverhill, Carlson Place and Indian Creek organized over the weekend, citing noise, property values and the strain on an Oncor grid they say already has long outages; rezoning documents say a planned Oncor substation on the site’s northern edge would supply the facility. Mayor Josh Schroeder noted on social media that this type of facility uses closed-loop cooling that consumes almost no water. City staff found the request meets three of five criteria for both a zoning amendment and a PUD (KVUE), and Texas Data Center Watch, citing the packet, counts zero written comments in favor and 32 opposed. If P&Z recommends approval, FOX 7 says City Council would make the final decision Dec. 8. Kyle’s council meeting is also tonight, where item 23 from Council Member Claudia Zapata would direct the city manager to bring back an ordinance that defines data centers and prohibits them in every zoning district. I’ll report both outcomes here tomorrow.

Office · Leasing & capital

Tesla is named as the tenant for 82,000 SF at 717 E. Parmer, as office absorption turns positive

Austin Business Journal · Nov 16, 2026 · The Registry · Bisnow · CBRE

Austin Business Journal reported Monday that several market reports link Tesla to a big office lease at 717 E. Parmer Lane, a former GM site, and that Tesla is hiring for finance and legal roles there, a shift from its recent industrial leasing. The Registry put the deal at 82,251 SF, the second-largest on Savills’ list of notable third-quarter leases, in a Northeast submarket where more than 40% of space is available and asking rents sit well below the metro average. The broader picture is improving: Bisnow, citing CBRE, says Austin office posted 253,000 SF of net absorption in Q3, including 124,000 SF of Class A, up from about 50,000 SF in Q2, with Class A asking rents averaging $58.54 per SF, up 2.5% year over year. Bisnow also covered Jay Paul’s $227.5 million C-PACE refinancing of the 881,000 SF Springdale Green campus, the largest C-PACE deal in Texas. For landlords along the Parmer corridor, a name like Tesla taking second-generation space is a useful signal.

Downtown · Hotel & condo

The Austin Lineage heads into historic review as a 993-foot Congress Avenue tower

Austin Business Journal · Nov 16, 2026 · Community Impact

Last week I covered the Lineage rezoning; this week the historic review package is out. Austin Business Journal reports Lanoha and architecture firm KPF are proposing The Austin Lineage, a 993-foot tower on Congress Avenue that would become the third-tallest building in Texas and the second-tallest in Austin behind Waterline. Community Impact describes a 71-story mixed-use tower at 400–408 Congress with a 221-room hotel, 87 condominiums and ground-level café and bar space, designed with House & Robertson Architects and under review because it sits in the Congress Avenue National Register Historic District. It’s a long road from review to groundbreaking, but it shows capital is still chasing the downtown hotel-and-residence product.

Industrial · Advanced manufacturing

“SCADA” tenants now lease 3.7 million SF here, and Aalo Atomics is eyeing another 606,000 SF in Georgetown

The Real Deal (citing Austin Business Journal) · Nov 13–15, 2026

The Real Deal’s weekend roundup gives a name to the tenants driving so much of our industrial absorption: SCADA, short for space, chips, advanced manufacturing, defense and AI. Citing ABJ, it says these companies lease 3.7 million SF of office and industrial space in the metro, up from 200,000 SF in 2015, and the ones needing big factory space are looking to Cedar Park, Hutto and Georgetown. Nuclear startup Aalo Atomics will occupy the 205,000 SF CrossPoint Building 2 at 4815 N. I-35 in Georgetown, part of Jackson Shaw’s 224-acre CrossPoint Business District, and is reportedly in talks for the 606,000 SF spec building next door, which LoopNet lists as available in March 2027. Lease terms weren’t disclosed.

Industrial · Southeast Austin

Amazon’s 1.2 million SF Project Toaster passed its filed start date; Project Juniper is next on Nov. 23

TDLR TABS record · Texas Gazette · Texas Construction Hub · Nov 2026

Monday, Nov. 16 was the filed start date for Amazon’s Project Toaster core and shell. The state accessibility record lists Amazon.com Services LLC as owner of a 1.2 million SF new-construction project at 475 Patton Ave. in Southeast Austin, estimated at $100 million with completion projected for July 13, 2027, covering office, factory, casting and a utility plant. A filed start date is the registrant’s projection, not confirmation that crews have mobilized. Nearby, Project Juniper, a 1,015,000 SF, $67 million warehouse at 11902 FM 969, has a filed start of Nov. 23; its tenant is still unnamed.

Last week · Condensed

Muny moves to Dec. 3, affordable deliveries, Burleson Road, Dell, Rooms To Go and two medical/retail trades

Austin Politics · Hoodline · The American Developer · Commercial Property Executive · Nov 12–14, 2026

Council’s vote on the UT agreement for Lions Municipal Golf Course is set for Dec. 3, and staff slides show UT’s developable floor area on roughly 190 acres going from about 1.7 million SF to about 4.9 million SF (Austin Politics). Rosewood Courts reopened with 184 affordable homes, Vecino’s 150-unit Cairn Point Cameron senior community opened, and Waverly North (76 units) is underway (Hoodline). A $10 million renovation of a 124,313 SF warehouse at 7051 Burleson Road carries a “Kendra Scott Warehouse” label with the tenant field “Not Assigned,” and Dell filed an $8.0 million renovation of Building 3 at 701 E. Parmer (The American Developer). Rooms To Go plans a roughly 49,182 SF showroom at Northline in Leander (Hoodline). Woodside Health bought the 55,000 SF Hills Medical Portfolio (CPE), and Gabriel’s Court traded to Realty Capital Partners and The Town Lake Company (Hoodline).
