Hays & Williamson · Data-center policy

Tuesday is a big data-center night: Kyle weighs a citywide ban while Georgetown hears Inner Loop Exchange

KVUE · Nov 12, 2026 · The American Developer · Nov 13, 2026 · Austin Business Journal · Nov 12, 2026

The I-35 corridor is splitting on data centers, and Nov. 17 shows it. In Kyle, KVUE reports Council Member Claudia Zapata is introducing an ordinance that would define data centers and prohibit them in every zoning district, modeled on the ban San Marcos passed in June. She told KVUE she isn’t aware of any data center currently proposed inside Kyle; she wants the city to act before one shows up. The American Developer’s read of the Nov. 17 agenda describes the item as direction for staff to draft that ordinance, and KVUE says it could come to a vote. The same evening, Georgetown’s Planning and Zoning Commission holds its 6 p.m. hearing on Inner Loop Exchange, the roughly 28-acre request for a data center of up to 290,000 SF. ABJ reported Thursday that a $1 billion data center is planned in Georgetown, with developers saying it could become the city’s largest taxpayer. For anyone holding land between Austin and San Antonio, the set of cities that permit this use is shrinking quickly, and that changes how sites get valued.

Retail · Neighborhood commercial

Gabriel’s Court changes hands for the first time in about 40 years, Cedar Park gets a $25M retail project, and Austin floats a rent buy-down for small businesses

Realty Capital Partners release (via Dallas Business Journal) · Austin Business Journal · Community Impact · Nov 13, 2026

Friday was a good day for smaller-format retail and office. Dallas-based Realty Capital Partners and Austin’s The Town Lake Company announced they bought Gabriel’s Court at 2905 San Gabriel Street near West 29th and North Lamar. It’s a 45,658 SF, three-story property with 24 office, medical and retail suites. Their release says it’s 88% occupied, averaged 92% over the past decade and hadn’t changed ownership in about 40 years. The new owners plan a multimillion-dollar refresh of common areas, signage, exterior and courtyard, and they’re betting on below-market rents near UT and Seton. Up in Cedar Park, ABJ reports a new retail development called TSSD III, estimated at $25 million, that could bring in coffee shops, gyms and restaurants. At City Hall, Community Impact reports that a resolution from Council Member Krista Laine, advanced in early November, asks Austin Economic Development to set up a publicly funded commercial rent buy-down program for local and legacy businesses. Funding and eligibility still have to be worked out with the business community first.

Permits · Pipeline

A 95-room hotel is permitted on Teri Road, and Project Juniper’s client is still unnamed ahead of its Nov. 23 start

The American Developer · Nov 13, 2026 · Hoodline (citing MySA) · Nov 13, 2026

The American Developer reports Austin issued permit 2020-079588 BP on Nov. 12 for a new five-story, 95-unit, 43,092 SF hotel at 1620 Teri Rd in Southeast Austin (78744). A&M Developers is listed as both applicant and contractor. The declared valuation is $4.0 million, which is the permit’s own job-value figure, and no brand or lender is named yet. On the industrial side, Hoodline, citing MySA, reports there’s still no named client for Project Juniper, the 1,015,000 SF, $67 million warehouse at 11902 FM 969, about three miles from Tesla’s headquarters. MySA calls it likely linked to Link Logistics based on earlier reporting, but the evidence doesn’t establish that it’s the same project as Juniper Crossing. The filed start is Nov. 23, and TDLR timelines can change.

Office · Capital markets

More on Springdale Green’s record $227.5 million C-PACE refinance

Commercial Observer · Nov 12, 2026 · Realty Today · Nov 13, 2026

A follow-up to yesterday’s lead office story: Jay Paul Company’s $227.5 million C-PACE loan from Nuveen Green Capital on the 881,427 SF Springdale Green campus at 1011 Springdale Road is the largest C-PACE deal in Texas history. It refinanced the construction loan and recapitalized efficiency upgrades that were already installed. Realty Today adds market context: Colliers put Austin office vacancy at 20.6% in Q2 2026, down from 22.3% a year earlier, and East Austin remains one of the higher-vacancy submarkets. That’s why patient, fixed-rate debt matters for a campus with space still to lease. Nuveen’s Cory Jubran pointed to roughly 1 million SF of Austin leasing over the past six months from technology, defense and chip tenants.

Industrial · Energy tech

Titan’s 525,000 SF Hutto spec building, Base Power’s ABIA headquarters, and Aalo Atomics in Georgetown

Austin Business Journal · The American Developer · Community Impact · Nov 10–12, 2026

Carrying over in brief: ABJ reports Titan is building another huge spec industrial building in Hutto. The state filing for “Hutto 10 Spec” describes about 525,000 SF at 2234 Limmer Loop, with an estimated cost of $33 million and a planned Jan. 8, 2027 start (The American Developer). Base Power plans a headquarters next to its “factory 2” site on Metropolis Drive near Austin-Bergstrom (ABJ). Nuclear startup Aalo Atomics is opening a manufacturing facility in Georgetown, and Community Impact’s read of the state filing describes a $25 million renovation of a 204,350 SF warehouse at 4813 N. I-35. Energy-tech users keep adding to big-box and flex demand.

Earlier this week · Condensed

Dog’s Head TIRZ shares, Mirada’s restart, Austin Lineage rezoning and 4T Ranch

Community Impact · Austin Business Journal · Texas Construction Hub · Nov 11–13, 2026

Austin formalized the share of future tax increment from the 2,645-acre Dog’s Head district that will go to housing and parks (5% over the first 20 years, 14% over the last 15), while the Defend Dog’s Head coalition gathers signatures to end the Endeavor agreement (Community Impact). Dallas-based RREAF is “trying to activate” Mirada, with 3,131 acres of mixed-use potential in Caldwell County (ABJ). The Austin Lineage (Project Bolo) rezoning at 402 and 408 Congress would move the site from DDB400 to DDB850, allowing up to 1,200 feet, for about 87 condos, 221 hotel rooms and a restaurant (Texas Construction Hub). The Robinson family is looking for a master developer for 1,376-acre 4T Ranch in Georgetown (ABJ).
