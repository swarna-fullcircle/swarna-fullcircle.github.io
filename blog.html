Data centers / adaptive reuse · Northwest Austin

Four Points town hall zeroes in on SoftBank’s former 3M campus — R&D campus or data center?

Community Impact · Sep 18, 2026 · also Austin American-Statesman

Inside city limits, the digital-infrastructure debate just got a very specific address. Community Impact reports dozens of Four Points residents met Sept. 17 with City of Austin officials and an SB Energy representative about the former 3M campus off River Place Boulevard — a Karlin Real Estate “Highpoint 2222” adaptive-reuse play that Travis County records show sold to SB Energy in early 2026. SB Energy’s Jaime Carlson framed Project Cosmos as renovating the site for U.K. semiconductor maker Graphcore’s U.S. engineering headquarters to research, develop, and test power-dense chips, with no zoning change, noise limits honored, closed-loop chillers aimed at holding water use at or below historic 3M levels, and electric capacity capped at 50 megawatts. Residents still pressed on wildfire risk, vibration, and whether the campus stays R&D or becomes a data center — an Aug. 31 SEC filing cited in coverage lists Cosmos as a data center. Separately, the Austin American-Statesman’s earlier SEC-filing read (SE Cosmos LLC; ~107 acres purchased in April) describes nearly $1 billion in financing to convert the roughly 1.1 million-square-foot office campus into an R&D technology center with about 50 megawatts of critical IT load, plus a SoftBank-affiliate lease expected to generate roughly $2.5 billion for data-center and office uses — all while Austin City Council works toward restricting large and hyperscale data centers in city limits. For me, this is the CRE collision: recycled campus inventory, substation upgrades, water benchmarking, and entitlement politics on one of Northwest Austin’s largest tech sites.

Hospitality / mixed-use · Downtown Austin

1 Hotel Austin is open inside Waterline — Texas’ tallest mixed-use tower

PR Newswire · Sep 17, 2026 · also Community Impact · KUT · REBusinessOnline

Downtown’s hospitality pipeline just flipped from construction to operating inventory. A Sept. 17 PR Newswire release from 1 Hotels marks the brand’s first Texas property as now open inside the 74-story Waterline tower — 252 rooms (including 60 suites), three dining concepts, Bamford Wellness Spa, and more than 10,000 square feet of meeting and event space along Waterloo Greenway. Community Impact earlier put the soft opening at Sept. 3 at the Red River / Confluence edge of Rainey Street. KUT’s Sept. 14 walk-through adds the tower’s office-and-residential stack and skyline context (Texas’ tallest building, over 1,000 feet). REBusinessOnline’s late-August completion write-up frames the CRE stack underneath: Lincoln Property Co. and Kairoi Residential’s roughly 2.7 million-square-foot tower at about 1,025 feet, with some 700,000 square feet of office, roughly 350 Bosque Residences apartments, a 1 Hotels flag, and about 24,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and restaurant space. For me, this is still the mixed-use delivery story — hotel keys, office product, multifamily, and street-level retail all coming online in one CBD landmark.

Industrial / rail logistics · Taylor

Partners Real Estate plans TaylorPort 3 — a 573,000 SF rail-served building at RCR

Taylor Press · Sep 18, 2026 · also Austin Business Journal

Northern MSA industrial keeps stacking next to Samsung. Taylor Press reports Partners Real Estate is advancing TaylorPort 3 — a planned 573,000-square-foot building with loading docks along the dedicated rail line at RCR Taylor Logistics Park — still in planning and design, with no construction start date disclosed yet. It would join Partners’ existing TaylorPort inventory on about 58 acres: a 183,000-square-foot rail-served building occupied by Tesla and a 366,000-square-foot building for Compal USA Technology. Managing director Mitchell Hanzik called TaylorPort 3 the largest of the three phases and said the newest building will occupy Partners’ last parcel at RCR, with the firm potentially looking for more Taylor-area land. Austin Business Journal separately highlighted the 573,000-square-foot rail-served product (cross-dock configuration, office pods, truck courts, heavy power) and Compal’s lease next door. For me, this is the supplier-and-logistics CRE read beside Samsung’s Taylor fab: speculative big-box inventory racing to catch institutional tenants before the park’s remaining parcels are gone.

Industrial / advanced manufacturing · Georgetown

Aalo Atomics plans a $25M advanced manufacturing build-out in Georgetown

Community Impact · Sep 17, 2026 · also Austin American-Statesman

Northern MSA industrial continues to pull advanced-manufacturing tenants into existing warehouse product. Community Impact reports Austin-based Aalo Atomics — which designs factory-built modular nuclear plants aimed at powering AI data centers — plans a $25 million renovation of a 204,350-square-foot one-story warehouse at 4813 N. I-35 in Georgetown into lab, machine-shop, and production-floor space. A Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation filing cited in coverage pegs construction from Nov. 15, 2026, through Jan. 30, 2028. The Austin American-Statesman separately framed the project as a roughly 200,000-square-foot production expansion about 40 miles north of Aalo’s East Ben White headquarters, after the company’s test reactor reached criticality in July. For me, this is the CRE read: adaptive reuse of large-bay industrial along the I-35 corridor, tying Georgetown warehouse inventory to the same power-and-AI demand story driving data-center land east of Austin.

Data centers / land · Taylor

Texas AG opens investigation into Taylor data center site tied to 1999 parkland deed

KUT · Sep 17, 2026 · also Texas Attorney General · Austin Business Journal

Entitlement risk just got louder on one of Taylor’s contested digital-infrastructure sites. KUT and a Sept. 17 Texas Attorney General press release report that Ken Paxton’s office is investigating whether a proposed Blueprint Projects AI data center can proceed on land conveyed in 1999 “to be held in trust for future use as parkland.” Coverage traces the chain: a conveyance to the Texas Parks and Recreation Foundation, later transfers to the City of Taylor and the Taylor Economic Development Corporation, then a 2025 sale of the property to NCP Travis TPP Project, LLC for $10 million. Blueprint’s planned facility is described as about 135,000 square feet with an on-site electricity substation; a residents’ lawsuit was previously dismissed for lack of standing and remains on appeal. Austin Business Journal separately flagged the state investigation on Sept. 18. For me, this is the CRE overlay: title history and charitable-trust questions can freeze or reshape a data-center land play even after local economic-development conveyances.

Retail / distress · North Austin

Galleria Oaks Chapter 11 filing stalls a North Austin retail foreclosure

Hoodline · Sep 18, 2026 · also Bondoro filing alert

Not every retail headline this month is a new pad lease. Hoodline, citing The Real Deal and court records, reports that WC Galleria Oaks Center, LLC — tied to Nate Paul’s World Class Holdings — filed Chapter 11 on Sept. 1, the same day the North Austin shopping center at 13376 North Highway 183 was scheduled for a foreclosure auction, securing a temporary stay. A Bondoro filing alert lists the case as No. 26-11754 in the Western District of Texas, with reported assets and liabilities each in the $10 million to $50 million range and Natin Paul as authorized agent. Coverage describes an 11-acre, roughly 55,000-square-foot center (tax-appraised around $12.8 million) with about 17 retail tenants and notes this is at least the second time an auction attempt on the property has been interrupted. For me, this is the other side of the CRE ledger: older strip product still carries refinance and ownership risk even when leasing elsewhere looks steadier.

Industrial / cleanroom · North Austin

Prudential plots a 100,000 SF North Austin cleanroom facility and 150 jobs

Austin Business Journal · Sep 17, 2026 · also Prudential Overall Supply

Cleanroom demand is translating into bricks-and-mortar expansion inside Austin proper — and the public groundbreaking is nearly here. Austin Business Journal reports Prudential Overall Supply is preparing a roughly 100,000-square-foot North Austin facility — a $40 million to $45 million investment expected to create about 150 jobs — as lab and cleanroom space becomes more common across the market. The company’s own groundbreaking notice points to 1001 West Howard Lane, with a public ribbon-cutting set for Oct. 1, 2026, and a Q1 2028 completion target for a combined cleanroom and industrial laundry plant (its 38th U.S. location and ninth ISO Class 3 cleanroom laundry facility). I’m reading this as the support-ecosystem story behind semiconductor, life-sciences, and advanced manufacturing tenants: when cleanroom inventory grows, the service footprint follows.

Land · Georgetown

Georgetown’s Yearwood MUD final vote is tomorrow — commercial corner on Reagan

Community Impact · Sep 14, 2026 · also Williamson County Sun

The northern MSA land pipeline stays on my calendar — final vote is tomorrow (Sept. 22). Community Impact reports Georgetown City Council gave initial approval Sept. 8 to establish an in-city municipal utility district for the Yearwood property — roughly 728 acres north of Sun City, west of Ronald Reagan Boulevard and Rattlesnake Road, long operated as a stone quarry. The Yearwood family, Freehold Capital Management, and the city have been negotiating since February. The plan is multiuse: majority residential (duplexes, townhomes, and 45-to-60-foot single-family lots, house count reduced to 1,728) with commercial land planned at the Reagan–Rattlesnake corner. In exchange for MUD consent, dedications include a 140-acre Berry Creek Park, trails, amenity centers, a 7-acre public-safety site, and a 110-foot-wide minor arterial linking Reagan northward. Final approval is slated for the Sept. 22 meeting — so I’m watching that commercial pad as the CRE piece of a much larger Georgetown entitlement story.
