Georgetown · Data centers

A $1 billion data center is planned in Georgetown, and the Inner Loop Exchange rezoning heads to P&Z on Nov. 17

Austin Business Journal · Nov 12, 2026 · KVUE · Nov 11, 2026

Data centers keep moving up I-35. The Austin Business Journal reported Thursday that a $1 billion data center is planned for Georgetown, and the developers told ABJ there’s a good chance it ends up being the city’s largest taxpayer. KVUE’s coverage fills in the zoning side of a proposal called Inner Loop Exchange on about 28 acres near Northeast Inner Loop and East University Avenue: the developer is seeking a zoning change to allow a data center of up to 290,000 SF, versus the 5,000 SF limit under the current commercial zoning. City staff found it meets three of five criteria for both a zoning amendment and a Planned Unit Development, while raising concerns about size and neighborhood fit. Developer flyers cited by KVUE describe a 68 MW facility with closed-loop cooling and power from Oncor rather than Georgetown’s municipal utility. Neighboring HOA leaders oppose the location, and the developer hadn’t responded to KVUE. ABJ’s description of the facility matches those flyers, but its full story is behind a paywall, so I’ll confirm the link between the two at the hearing. Georgetown’s Planning and Zoning Commission takes it up Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 6 p.m.

Office · Capital markets

Springdale Green lands a $227.5 million C-PACE refinance, the largest in Texas history

Commercial Observer · Nov 12, 2026

This is a big office financing story. Commercial Observer reports Jay Paul Company closed $227.5 million in C-PACE debt from Nuveen Green Capital to refinance the construction loan on its 881,427 SF Springdale Green office campus at 1011 Springdale Road in East Austin. It’s the largest C-PACE loan in Texas history, coming a decade after the state’s first TX-PACE deal, and it recapitalizes energy and water efficiency measures already in place. Nuveen’s Cory Jubran said the long-term fixed rate gives the sponsor time to lease up a campus that was substantially completed a little under two years ago. He also noted roughly 1 million SF of Austin office leasing over the last six months by technology, defense and chip tenants. For owners with newer, partly leased buildings, it’s a useful example of financing that doesn’t depend on a quick sale.

Industrial · Big-box spec

Titan plans another huge spec building in Hutto: 525,000 SF on Limmer Loop

Austin Business Journal · Nov 12, 2026 · The American Developer · Nov 11, 2026 · Texas Construction Hub · Nov 12, 2026

ABJ reports Titan is building another massive speculative industrial building in Hutto, noting that large product across the metro is getting leased quickly by tech, aerospace and defense users. The state filing behind it, covered by The American Developer, is TDLR registration TABS2027005552 for “Hutto 10 Spec”: a one-story tilt-up warehouse of about 525,000 SF at 2234 Limmer Loop with a $33 million estimated cost, a planned Jan. 8, 2027 start and Nov. 15, 2027 completion. No tenant or design firm is named. Texas Construction Hub adds context on Project Juniper, the 1,015,000 SF, $67 million warehouse at 11902 FM 969 I covered yesterday. The filing names B9 FM969 Owner, the same filer registered the Hutto spec building that day, and the address matches the site Blackstone’s Link Logistics planned in 2024 as Juniper Crossing. TCH notes that connection hasn’t been confirmed. Juniper’s filed start is still Nov. 23, and both dates are the registrants’ projections.

Energy tech · Occupiers

Base Power plans a large HQ near ABIA, and Aalo Atomics sets up in Georgetown

Austin Business Journal · Nov 12 & Nov 10, 2026 · Community Impact

Energy companies keep turning into real estate users. ABJ reports Base Power plans a headquarters next to its “factory 2” site on Metropolis Drive near Austin-Bergstrom, and that the HQ could hold thousands of employees. Up in Georgetown, ABJ reported this week that nuclear startup Aalo Atomics will open a manufacturing facility there. Community Impact’s earlier read of the state filing describes a $25 million renovation of a 204,350 SF one-story warehouse at 4813 N. Land · Master plans

Austin sets Dog’s Head TIRZ shares as a petition drive starts; RREAF works to restart Mirada; Yardly heads to Easton Park

Community Impact · Nov 12, 2026 · Austin Business Journal · Nov 12, 2026

Community Impact reports that on Nov. 5 Council formalized how future tax increment from the 2,645-acre Dog’s Head district will support housing and parks. The TIRZ will set aside 5% of property and sales tax revenue over its first 20 years (projected at more than $270 million) and 14% over its last 15 years (nearly $1.5 billion), split evenly between the Housing Trust Fund and Parks Enhancement Fund. The district’s value could reach nearly $27 billion by the 2060s on Endeavor’s schedule, and Amazon’s 1.2 million SF, $100 million robotics facility is underway as anchor tenant. Updated development-agreement and mobility plans go to public review starting in December, ahead of Council consideration in March. The Defend Dog’s Head coalition is collecting signatures to end the Endeavor agreement; under Prop G, any such measure couldn’t reach voters before November 2028. Elsewhere, ABJ reports Dallas-based RREAF is “trying to activate” Mirada, with 3,131 acres of mixed-use potential in Caldwell County, after a years-long freeze. Community Impact says Taylor Morrison’s Yardly expects to break ground this month on 161 build-to-rent homes at Easton Park, with leasing in late 2027.

Earlier this week · Condensed

Austin Lineage rezoning, Formation’s first Austin buy, River Park, 4T Ranch and ERCOT’s data-center ask

Community Impact · KVUE · Texas Construction Hub · CPE · CBS Austin · ABJ · McGregor Brief · Nov 10–12, 2026

Carrying over in brief: the Austin Lineage (Project Bolo) at 400 Congress is described in city filings as a 71-floor, 993-foot tower with 221 hotel rooms and about 87 condos.
