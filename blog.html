Pipeline / permits · City of Austin

Austin permitted 2,863 new apartment units in Q3 — up 55% from a year ago — with two projects carrying most of the declared value

The American Developer · Oct 13, 2026

My first read this Wednesday is a clean supply signal from City of Austin records. The American Developer reports that new-construction building permits for apartments issued between July 1 and Sept. 30, 2026 covered 2,863 units across 12 properties — up 55% from 1,852 units on the same query for Q3 2025 — with about $477 million in declared job value across the 10 properties that stated a value. The mix is lopsided: 512 Sabine Street (696 units, Ryan Companies) and 2631 Kramer Lane / The Kent (396 units, OHT Construction) together hold 38% of the units and roughly 90% of the declared value. Q4 is already moving — a 397-unit permit at 2300 Kramer Lane was issued Oct. 9. For brokers underwriting lease-up, this is a permit pipeline, not a guarantee of on-time deliveries, but it is the clearest City-only quarter count I’ve seen this week.

Data centers / filings · Hutto and Cedar Creek

Two data-center shells file $916 million in estimated construction: Skybox Hutto 4 at $550M and Project Longhorn Building One in Cedar Creek at $366M

The American Developer · Oct 13, 2026 · Community Impact · Oct 13

East and southeast of Austin, the TDLR Architectural Barriers (TABS) register is loud. The American Developer reports that Skybox Hutto 4, registered Sept. 30 at 3399 County Road 132 in Hutto, lists a $550 million estimated cost for a core-and-shell data center (scope text describes about 844,300 square feet of data center plus about 20,000 square feet of office), with a Jan. 20, 2027 start and Oct. 30, 2028 completion, Corgan Associates as design firm. Seven days later, Project Longhorn Building One in Cedar Creek (Earl Callahan Road near Walter Hoffman Road) registered Oct. 7 at $366 million estimated cost for about 498,165 gross square feet, with a Nov. 30, 2026 start and Jan. 31, 2028 completion, Gensler as design firm. Community Impact’s Oct. 13 metro permit roundup also flags Project Longhorn at that $366 million figure and, closer to my Leander office, a Rooms To Go at 10021 183A Toll Road with a $5.8 million estimated cost and a 2027 construction window. Those are registrant estimates, not loans — but the start dates put civil and steel packages on a near-term clock.

Today / water policy · Statewide and Taylor

Today is the TWDB’s Oct. 14 checkpoint with the governor on data-center water-survey enforcement; no public update was posted overnight, while Taylor’s campus agreement already locks in reclaimed-water cooling

Office of the Texas Governor · Sept 14 · City of Taylor project page · KUT · Oct 9

As those filings land, today’s calendar item is state water oversight. Gov. Greg Abbott’s Sept. 14 directive requires the Texas Water Development Board to update the governor’s office by Oct. 14 on enforcement of data-center water-use survey compliance, including coordination with ERCOT’s audit work. As of this morning’s refresh I have not seen a public TWDB progress release for that deadline, so I’m treating today as the checkpoint rather than a finished report. On the local side, after Taylor City Council’s Oct. 8 approval, the City of Taylor’s project page for the 664-acre Taylor Technology Campus spells out closed-loop cooling with reclaimed water (initial fill 1.575 million gallons for the entire site), Chapter 29 wastewater design, and withdrawal of the developers’ TCEQ wastewater discharge permit application — terms KUT also covered after the unanimous annexation agreement vote. For landowners along US 79, underwriting now includes both city covenants and whether TWDB’s enforcement update surfaces publicly in the days ahead.

Multifamily investment · South Austin

ORIX Corp. USA buys Strata, a 322-unit luxury community on Bluff Springs Road, with a $42 million acquisition loan from Franklin BSP

YieldPro · Oct 12, 2026 · Connect CRE

I’m still carrying yesterday’s south-Austin trade because institutional buying of a newly delivered asset remains this week’s clearest investment signal. YieldPro reports that ORIX Corp. USA purchased Strata, a 322-unit luxury property at 8340 Bluff Springs Road, from Maverick Development Group, with Franklin BSP Realty Trust issuing a $42 million acquisition loan maturing in 2031. Strata came online last year as the penultimate stage of Ethos, a six-phase project planned for more than 2,000 units. The community has 12 buildings with studio, one-, and two-bedroom layouts averaging 875 square feet, plus a business lounge with private work pods, gym, pool, and community room, about 10 miles southwest of downtown and less than a mile from I-35. Connect CRE’s write-up matches the buyer, unit count, and loan details.

Affordable housing / rehab · East Austin

Austin advances up to $33 million in financing to renovate Elm Ridge, a 130-unit affordable complex on Airport Boulevard

Austin Business Journal · Oct 12, 2026 · City of Austin / AHFC notice · Sept 17

Alongside market-rate trades, preservation capital on the east side still matters. The Austin Business Journal reports that City Council approved up to $33 million in bonds for Elm Ridge Preservation LP to renovate the 130-unit Elm Ridge Apartments at 1190 Airport Boulevard. An Austin Housing Finance Corporation public-hearing notice described notes whose proceeds would be loaned for leasing, acquisition, rehabilitation, and equipping of the project for low-income individuals and families, with a maximum aggregate face amount of $33 million. In a market still digesting new supply, keeping older affordable stock online is as consequential as entitling the next tower.

Groundbreaking · Southwest Austin

The Barton breaks ground in Oak Hill: 402 homes including 30 townhomes on about 60 acres along West SH 71, targeting early-2028 first deliveries

Texas Construction Hub · Oct 12, 2026

Southwest of downtown, another multifamily start joins the pipeline. Texas Construction Hub reports that The Barton has broken ground at 8956 West State Highway 71 in Oak Hill — 402 homes including 30 townhomes on roughly 60 acres, with more than 40 acres described as green space. The plan pairs two- and three-story buildings with townhomes; initial homes are targeted for early 2028 and broader community completion in late 2028. Amenities called out in the covered announcement include pools, a clubhouse, fitness and coworking space, and trails. No overall construction value is established in the accounts reviewed, so I’m not inventing one — the useful next markers are first verticals, utility connections, and a leasing sequence.

Still in play · From this week

Carrying forward: CityView’s 475-unit Loop 360 redevelopment, Leland’s 45% pre-sales, Georgetown’s William’s Landing retail sale, and Vertiv’s 120,000-SF-plus Logistics Park occupancy

Groundbreak · Oct 11 · Statesman · Oct 11 · Marcus & Millichap · Sept 30 / Oct 5 · Texas Construction Hub · Oct 8

A few stories from the past several days still frame the week. Groundbreak reports AREIT City View LLC won approval to redevelop the CityView office complex at 1120 and 1122 South Capital of Texas Highway into roughly 475 mixed-use residential units under DB90. The Statesman reports Intracorp’s Leland South Congress is underway on a $173 million BGO construction loan with 45% of its 265 condos already pre-sold. Closer to home, Marcus & Millichap announces the all-cash sale of William’s Landing at 3920 Williams Drive in Georgetown — a 2025-built 14,777-square-foot, eight-suite retail center on 1.87 acres — to an out-of-state institutional buyer, and Texas Construction Hub reports Vertiv has expanded past 120,000 square feet at Stonelake’s Georgetown Logistics Park near I-35 and SH 130. Office-to-residential repositioning, South Congress condo demand, and north-metro retail and industrial occupancy are all moving at once.
