Data centers / industrial land · Hutto / Williamson County

Skybox Hutto 4 moves to approved — Prologis’ 846,300 SF core-and-shell building on CR 132, even as ABJ flags a power-driven industrial pivot for the megasite

Groundbreak Austin · Oct 5, 2026 · also Austin Business Journal · Oct 2 · Taylor Press · Oct 2 · Data Center Dynamics · Oct 2

The Hutto megasite gets a status change to start the week. Groundbreak Austin reports Oct. 5 that Prologis has received approval for Skybox Hutto 4, a single-story, 846,300-square-foot data center at 3399 County Road 132 in Hutto, designed by Corgan Associates. It is filed as industrial use under infrastructure, built as core and shell with tenant fit-out handled separately, and completion is expected in late October 2028. That lines up with Data Center Dynamics’ Oct. 2 report on the state filing (construction January 2027 through October 2028, a $550 million investment figure, on a PowerCampus planned for 600 MW of IT capacity). The tension I’m watching is power: Austin Business Journal’s Justin Sayers reported Oct. 2 that the broader Skybox–Prologis plan is pivoting toward industrial uses because securing ERCOT power has grown “more challenging,” and Taylor Press covered Hutto City Council’s Oct. 1 meeting, where Skybox said power could take another two years before the next phase starts and council rezoned a 140-acre parcel with data-center and power-station pre-approval but no tenant yet. Approvals are moving faster than electrons on the northeastern growth ring, and that gap is what prices this land.

Data centers / annexation · Taylor / Williamson County

Taylor’s Big Watt–PowerHouse annexation vote is this Thursday — 664 acres, closed-loop reclaimed water, $28.2M community package

KUT · Oct 2, 2026 · also City of Taylor · Oct 2 · Austin Business Journal · Oct 2 · The Real Deal Texas · Oct 4

Next door, the vote I’ve been flagging is now days away. KUT reported Oct. 2 that Taylor is considering an agreement with Big Watt Digital and PowerHouse Data Centers to annex the planned 664-acre Taylor Technology Campus (formerly Project Mustang) between FM 112 and US 79, with council set to vote Oct. 8. The city’s project page lays out the terms: closed-loop cooling on reclaimed water, developer-paid utility connections, setbacks of at least 300 feet on three sides and 700 feet along the south line near FM 112, a noise cap, and $28.2 million in community investments. City materials project about $1.38 billion in gross tax revenue over 20 years with no abatement request, and ABJ cites $4.8 billion in economic output to Williamson County over two decades. The politics are live, too: The Real Deal Texas’ Oct. 4 recap of the Texas Tribune Festival notes that the mere mention of data centers drew boos on a property-tax panel, even as panelists argued they could ease the homeowner tax burden.

Healthcare development · South Austin

St. David’s is under construction on a $180M, 160,000 SF patient tower at its Ben White campus

Groundbreak Austin · Oct 3, 2026

Medical construction keeps showing up in the pipeline. Groundbreak Austin reports Oct. 3 that St. David’s South Austin Medical Center’s new patient tower at 901 W. Ben White Blvd. has moved from proposed to active construction: a $180 million, four-story addition of 160,000 square feet, designed by Polkinghorn Group Architects. The program includes a 30-bed patient care unit, a 20-bed inpatient rehab unit, four more operating rooms with shell space for future expansion, a new utility powerhouse, and sterile processing, with completion set for the end of 2027. For me, hospital campus expansion is one of the steadiest generators of nearby medical office and service retail demand along the US 290/71 frontage.

Mixed-use / waterfront · South Central Waterfront

Related starts its 18-story One Lady Bird Lake tower — 298 residences, two waterfront restaurants, Feb. 2028 target

Groundbreak Austin · Oct 4, 2026

The South Central Waterfront has a crane story. Groundbreak Austin reports Oct. 4 that construction is underway on One Lady Bird Lake, Related Companies’ 18-story, 200-foot mixed-use tower at 151 S. 1st Street, designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox. The project totals 696,025 square feet with 298 condominiums and apartments (each with its own lobby), 463 parking spaces, two waterfront restaurants, and a connection to the Lady Bird Lake boardwalk, with completion expected in February 2028. I’m watching it as the ground-floor restaurant and retail piece of a district that has mostly lived on paper; a delivered anchor there resets what nearby sites can underwrite.

Retail development · Buda / Hays County

H-E-B sets a $61.2M store on a long-vacant former landfill site off I-35 in Buda

MySA · Oct 4, 2026

Grocery-anchored retail is still chasing rooftops south along I-35. MySA’s Cristela Jones reports Oct. 4 that H-E-B has set plans for a nearly $61.2 million store at 15300 S. I-35 in Buda, aiming to turn a long-vacant former landfill into a new retail destination anchored by the store. The same roundup notes smaller restaurant filings in Buda (Layne’s Chicken Fingers in a former Zaxby’s and Calacas Tacos’ first Central Texas location) and the city’s Sportsplex finishing its first construction phase in September. Reuse of a hard site for a grocery anchor tells me how scarce well-located I-35 frontage has become between Austin and San Marcos.

Retail / restaurant leasing · Central Austin

Grubhub parent Wonder targets The Triangle for a $900K food hall buildout and is seeking more Austin sites

Austin Business Journal · Oct 4, 2026

A new restaurant-tech user is shopping Austin space. Austin Business Journal’s Sahar Chmais reports Oct. 4 that Wonder, the food hall and delivery company that owns Grubhub, has filed plans for two stores in the city, including a roughly $900,000 buildout at The Triangle, and is looking for more locations. Full details sit behind ABJ’s paywall, so I’d check the story before quoting specifics. For landlords with second-generation restaurant or small-box retail space, delivery-heavy formats like this are a different kind of tenant than a traditional sit-down operator.

Infrastructure · Austin-Bergstrom International Airport

AUS Concourse M shrinks to 37,357 SF and slides to a March 2028 finish

Groundbreak Austin · Oct 3, 2026

An airport scope change worth noting for anyone tracking the AUS area. Groundbreak Austin reports Oct. 3 that Concourse M at Austin-Bergstrom has been registered as a $10 million project with planned square footage cut from 84,500 to 37,357 square feet and completion moved from the end of 2026 to March 2028. The three-story facility will serve walk-out gates and include a coffee market, grab-and-go concessions, retail, and passenger amenities. Gate and concession capacity at the airport feeds directly into hotel, logistics, and service demand around the southeast submarket, so timing slips matter.

Occupier demand · Austin MSA

Doodle Labs is still hunting Austin space — WeWork for now, hybrid office-plus-testing next, with SoCo in the mix

The Real Deal Texas · Oct 2, 2026 · also Austin Business Journal · Oct 1

I’m keeping one live tenant requirement on the board. The Real Deal Texas reported Oct. 2 (citing Austin Business Journal) that defense-tech company Doodle Labs has moved its U.S. headquarters from the Los Angeles area to the Austin metro, is working out of a WeWork, and is searching for a hybrid office and testing location, possibly across multiple sites, with the South Congress corridor of particular interest. ABJ’s Oct. 1 piece says local headcount could more than quintuple by the end of 2027. That reads to me as flex and small-to-mid bay demand with a testing overlay, not just another HQ announcement.
