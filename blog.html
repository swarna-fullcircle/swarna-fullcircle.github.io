Austin CRE Market Update — July 2026

ECR · Aug 10, 2026

Capital is committing again across asset classes. Highlights: Hines paid $151M ($733/SF) for fully leased 405 Colorado — the highest downtown office PSF since 2021; Tesla leased 682,000 SF at Austin Hills Commerce Center (largest speculative industrial box in the region); Austin startups raised about $5B in H1 2026; office absorption remains strong while average footprints get smaller.

Data centers

Central Texas data center boom is repricing land and power

Propmodo · Mar 31, 2026

Power access — not just location — is becoming the main driver of land value and industrial site desirability. The story is no longer Austin-only: growth stretches from Temple through Round Rock, Hutto, Georgetown, Taylor, and toward San Antonio, reshaping industrial and infrastructure-adjacent CRE.

SoftBank affiliate details plans for former 3M Austin campus

Austin American-Statesman

SE Cosmos LLC (an SB Energy / SoftBank-linked affiliate) that bought the 107-acre former 3M campus outlined R&D and data-center-related uses in an SEC filing — even as Austin leaders move toward restricting large and hyperscale data centers inside city limits.

Pipeline

Austin office and apartment permit pipeline nearly dried up

Transparent.city · 2026

New permits for offices, large apartments, and retail have collapsed versus 2025 — only 1 office building permit through mid-August 2026 (vs. 17 in all of 2025), and large multifamily applications down sharply. A quieter construction pipeline may help rebalance supply over time.

Multifamily

Austin multifamily stabilizes as construction cools

CRE Daily · citing Colliers Q2 2026

Occupancy rose 70 bps from Q1 even as inventory growth slowed. Q2 absorption (5,106 units) outpaced new supply (2,793). Rents remain soft near-term, with Colliers expecting gradual recovery into 2027 as deliveries slow.
