Industrial · Market data

Partners’ Q3 industrial report: vacancy dips to 15.5% as leasing more than doubles

Partners Real Estate · Q3 2026 · Realty News Report · Nov 7, 2026

Starting the week with the numbers. Partners Real Estate reports Austin industrial net absorption of 2,212,394 SF in Q3, vacancy down 24 basis points to 15.5%, leasing velocity up 155.9% quarter over quarter to 4.3 million SF, and a construction pipeline of 14.0 million SF, concentrated in Bastrop (3.1 million SF) and Caldwell (2.0 million SF) counties. Georgetown was the strongest submarket at 773,235 SF absorbed. Named deals include CoreWeave’s 578,000 SF lease in Bastrop County, Tesla’s 538,720 SF at Mustang Ridge, and Skybox Datacenters’ 469,000 SF at PowerCampus Austin in Round Rock. Note that this differs from the Savills figures I shared Saturday (16.1% vacancy) — different methodologies, same direction of travel. Realty News Report adds that Partners represented AI Prefab Solutions in a 196,523 SF lease at 2100 Limmer Loop in Hutto and SH 130 Concession Co. in buying 25 acres near SH 130 and SH 80 in Lockhart.

Data centers · Regulation & infrastructure

STEERS is still down, Hays County’s data-center pause runs through year-end, and Austin’s Dec. 3 vote is on track

TCEQ · Hays Free Press · City of Austin memo

I rechecked the follow-up from last week: TCEQ’s STEERS page now loads and says the system was shut down so staff can review every data-center application under the Governor’s Sept. 21 directive, with the fix still anticipated in mid-November. TCEQ extended the MSGP renewal deadline to Dec. 28 and is accepting email submissions for certain general permits in the meantime. Locally, Hays County’s 180-day resolution defers discretionary approvals and incentives for large industrial water users, including data centers, through Dec. 31, and the Austin staff memo still shows a Nov. 20 agenda posting and Dec. 3 Council consideration of Phase 1 rules. I couldn’t confirm the outcome of Hays’ development-regulations adoption, which could come as soon as Tuesday.

Round Rock & Georgetown · Corporate and industrial

Dell’s $12M server-lab upgrade in Round Rock and a $42M Protec PV buildout in Georgetown

The American Developer · Nov 7, 2026 and Oct 28, 2026 · Connect CRE

The American Developer notes Dell USA LP registered a $12.0M, 6,145 SF mechanical/electrical and server-rack project at 701 E. Office · Deals and leasing

DML buys a 123K SF office building; SolarWinds takes 100K SF at Zilker Point; Meta and Indeed lead sublease blocks

Connect CRE · Nov 2 and Oct 21, 2026 · Austin Business Journal · Nov 6, 2026

Connect CRE reports DML Capital acquired a 123,272 SF Austin office building from Brick Row, and that SolarWinds signed a 100,000 SF lease at Zilker Point. Both show tenants and buyers still moving on quality or well-priced space. Meanwhile ABJ’s roundup of seven sublease blocks over 150,000 SF — led by Meta and Indeed, with the two largest downtown — is a reminder of the discounted supply available to users.

Downtown & multifamily · Permits and completions

Convention Center Area 1 permit, The Grant, and what’s under way around the core

Groundbreak · Nov 6–9, 2026 · The American Developer · Nov 7, 2026

Condensing Saturday’s lead: the city issued the permit for the 373,715 SF Convention Center Area 1 building (JE Dunn), with an $812M declared valuation shared across four permits — a project-wide figure, not a contract price. Ryan Companies’ 232-unit The Grant at 512 Sabine is permitted at an estimated $79.5M. Groundbreak’s latest entries add The Payton, a 176-unit fully affordable project on Payton Gin Road now under construction (Spring 2028), the 297-unit St. Johns West complete in Highland, a $2M renovation for a 15,000 SF members club, The Portal Austin, at 601 Rio Grande (January 2027), and 62 East, a 57-story condo tower on Rainey, targeting November 2027.
