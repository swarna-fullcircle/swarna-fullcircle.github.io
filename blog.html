Election / data centers · Statewide & Austin MSA

Texas elects Gina Hinojosa governor, putting her proposed data-center moratorium front and center for our market

Bisnow · Nov 4, 2026 · City of Hutto · Nov 3, 2026 meeting · Texas Construction Hub · Nov 2, 2026

Bisnow reports that The Associated Press named Austin state Rep. Gina Hinojosa the projected winner for Texas governor and James Talarico the projected winner for U.S. Senate, and that Gov. Greg Abbott called Hinojosa to concede at about 10 p.m. CT Tuesday. For commercial real estate, Bisnow says all eyes will turn to Hinojosa’s plan for a data-center development moratorium, stricter water and power rules, and an end to industry tax breaks, noting Texas now has more data centers under development than any other state. Closer to home, Hutto P&Z held its hearing last night on the 97.866-acre, six-lot Hutto Data Center Campus Subdivision One replat north and east of CR 132; I haven’t yet seen a published outcome, and the notice still doesn’t name a developer or operator. Austin’s Phase 1 data-center rules remain on track for a Dec. 3 Council vote. My read: anyone underwriting powered land in Hutto, Taylor or Bastrop should now be pricing in policy risk at the state level, not just the city level.

Office · South Austin

Spec suites pay off: 801 Barton Springs is nearly fully leased

Austin Business Journal · Nov 3, 2026

ABJ reports 801 Barton Springs is nearly entirely leased after its owners built out 10 spec suites and improved the exterior and ground floor. The new owners renegotiated WeWork’s full-building lease so the coworking firm now occupies just two floors, and the freed-up space drew significant interest, with SomeraRoad and Brick Row among the recent tenants. My read: in a market still near 25% vacancy, move-in-ready small suites in walkable locations keep winning.

Deals · Burnet Road, Northcross & Dripping Springs

90Ten buys a Burnet Road strip center, Northcross redevelopment is proposed, and ORIX buys a 1,029-unit portfolio

Groundbreak · Nov 2–3, 2026 · Austin Business Journal · Nov 2, 2026

Groundbreak reports 90Ten acquired a 14,260-SF retail strip center at 5335–5341 Burnet Rd., adding to its Burnet Road corridor portfolio, and that a Northcross Mall redevelopment has been proposed at 2525 W. Anderson Lane. In multifamily, ORIX USA acquired a three-property, 1,029-unit portfolio for roughly $200 million, including the 241-unit Lookout in Dripping Springs. That follows ABJ’s report that Weidner bought the 341-unit Solaris at the Domain, which ABJ frames as a sign the multifamily market could slowly be recovering.

Industrial & manufacturing · Taylor, Georgetown, Round Rock & Kyle

Samsung sets a November groundbreaking for Taylor Fab 2, and Amazon plans a Georgetown sortation center

Groundbreak · Oct 30–31, 2026 · ECR · Oct 27, 2026

Per Groundbreak, Samsung Austin Semiconductor will break ground in November on Fab 2, a 2.7 million-SF chip plant at 1530 FM 973 in Taylor, with production targeted for 2030. Amazon plans a 248,687-SF sorting warehouse at 104 Wittera Way in Georgetown, with completion scheduled for July 2027; a $20 million PulseForge expansion was approved in Round Rock; and Phase 3 of Dacy Business Park (43,050 SF) broke ground in Kyle. ECR’s Q3 read is that Austin industrial absorbed about 2.6 million SF while the pipeline (about 7.1 million SF) is less than half its size a year ago, and that rents haven’t moved yet, so tenants still have room to negotiate.

Groundbreak: Austin · ECR: Q3 industrial

Mixed-use & land · Georgetown

Georgetown approves 700-plus-unit Serenity Ranch, and Highland Homes self-develops 614 lots

Williamson County Sun · Nov 3, 2026 · Groundbreak · Nov 1, 2026 · citybiz · Nov 2, 2026

The Williamson County Sun reports Georgetown City Council approved Serenity Ranch on Oct. 27, a MUD in the ETJ near County Road 150 northwest of Weir with more than 700 multi- and single-family units plus commercial development, despite two council members’ concerns about public benefits and the proposed tax rate. Groundbreak reports Highland Homes is developing 614 lots across Melina and East View Farms, its first self-developed Austin-area projects. Southwestern University 560 is also under construction, led by a 75,000-SF city Customer Service Center, and Heirloom breaks ground Thursday.
