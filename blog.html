Multifamily · Investment

Knightvest buys 1,027-unit North Austin multifamily portfolio

REBusinessOnline · Sep 3, 2026

Capital is still rotating into Austin apartments. Newmark arranged the sale of three North Austin communities totaling 1,027 units — The Enclave at La Frontera (411), Lakeside at La Frontera (366), and Legends Lake Creek (250) — from San Antonio–based LYND to Knightvest Capital. The early-2000s assets average about 936 SF per unit. Patton Jones and Andrew Dickson represented the seller; Tip Strickland and David Schwarz arranged buyer financing. This is the kind of mid-vintage portfolio trade I’m watching as investors re-enter after the supply overhang.

Hospitality · Downtown

1 Hotel opens inside Waterline — first major tenant in Texas’ tallest tower

CBS Austin · Sep 3, 2026

Waterline’s hospitality stack is live. 1 Hotel Austin is open as the first major tenant in the 1,025-foot downtown tower, bringing restaurants, a rooftop pool, and more than 300 jobs. The brand leaned hard into sustainability (about 4,000 plants, reclaimed materials, filtered water in every room). For CRE, this is the next chapter after delivery: can office and luxury residential lease into a selective CBD while hospitality and tourism demand keep filling the mixed-use stack?

Office · Downtown

Hines buys 405 Colorado — fully leased CBD tower at a record PSF

REBusinessOnline · Aug 31, 2026

Trophy office is trading again. An affiliate of Houston-based Hines purchased 405 Colorado, a 206,000-square-foot downtown tower fully leased at closing to tenants including JPMorgan Chase, Bain & Co., and AllianceBernstein. REBusinessOnline, citing the Austin Business Journal, reports Brandywine Realty Trust sold the building for $151 million. ECR later noted that works out to about $733 per square foot — the richest downtown office PSF since the 2021 peak. Amenitized, income-backed CBD product is still what I’m watching as capital re-enters selectively.

Data centers · Land & power

Propmodo: Central Texas data centers are repricing land and industrial sites

Propmodo

Power is the new location. Propmodo walks through how Austin’s data-center boom is spreading from Temple through Round Rock, Pflugerville, Hutto, Georgetown, Taylor, and down toward San Antonio — with Colliers classifying Austin as a secondary U.S. market (645 MW size, 0.6% vacancy) and a much larger planned corridor. Powered land can trade at large premiums over ordinary industrial parcels, and entitlement/community pushback is now part of underwriting. For MSA land and industrial clients, this corridor thesis is hard to ignore.

Industrial · Georgetown

Aalo Atomics plans 200,000 SF manufacturing build-out in Georgetown

Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo Finance · Sep 2, 2026

I’m still watching the north corridor. Austin-based Aalo Atomics finalized plans for a more than 200,000-square-foot production and manufacturing facility in Georgetown — about 40 miles north of its East Ben White headquarters — tied to modular nuclear plants serving power-hungry data centers. A TDLR filing points to roughly $25 million to renovate a one-story warehouse off I-35 (lab, machine shop, production floor), with work expected to start Nov. 15 and wrap by January 2028.

Pipeline · Multifamily

Austin apartment permits nearly dried up through mid-2026

Transparent.city · Jul 22, 2026

The forward supply signal is stark. Transparent.city reports developers filed permits for just eight buildings with five or more units in Austin through July 20, 2026 — down from 38 in the same stretch of 2025 (a 79% drop). Six of those eight sit in District 4; two in District 9; every other district filed zero. Broader commercial construction permits were down 34% YoY. Paired with this week’s North Austin portfolio sale, I’m watching how a thinning 2027–28 pipeline may reshape multifamily pricing and leasing.

Office · Capital markets

JLL arranges refinance for fully leased Rollingwood III

REBusinessOnline · Sep 3, 2026

Southwest office still pencils for lenders when occupancy is solid. JLL arranged a fixed-rate loan (amount undisclosed) through Ascentris for Rollingwood III — a 121,115 SF, 2019-built office building at MoPac and Bee Caves Road within the 13-acre Rollingwood Center campus. The building was fully leased at the time of the deal. Colby Mueck and Doug Opalka represented the undisclosed borrower. Another data point that quality, leased suburban product can still clear the capital stack.

Data centers · Adaptive reuse

SoftBank affiliate details plans for former 3M Austin campus

I’m still tracking the former 3M campus in northwest Austin.
