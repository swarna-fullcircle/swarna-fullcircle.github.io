Office & R&D · Airport corridor

Zimmer Biomet takes 60,000 SF at MetCenter near the airport, and American Airlines is doubling its ABIA lounge

Austin Business Journal · Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation · Nov 24, 2026

The Austin Business Journal reports that medtech company Zimmer Biomet signed a lease for about 60,000 SF at 7551 Metro Center Dr. in MetCenter, near Austin-Bergstrom, for R&D and office use. ABJ calls it a significant local expansion for the company. The state filing gives a few more details: TDLR project TABS2027005319 describes a 60,000 SF interior fit-out on two floors of MetCenter 10, with an estimated cost of $5 million, paid for by the tenant, and dates from Dec. 1, 2026, to April 1, 2027. The owner on that filing is BGOGC Met Center 10 Owner LP. At the airport itself, ABJ reports that American Airlines is moving ahead with a lounge expansion that doubles its footprint at ABIA and adds an outdoor terrace with downtown views. That comes on top of last week’s $60 million Concourse M permit. Most of these stories are behind ABJ’s paywall, so I’m only sharing what the public summaries say. Still, the Southeast Austin airport submarket keeps drawing real occupiers, not just flights.

Downtown redevelopment · Mixed use

Millennium Partners makes the TownLake YMCA its first Texas project and is targeting a 2028 construction start

Austin Business Journal · Greater Austin YMCA · Nov 24, 2026

ABJ reports that New York-based Millennium Partners is using the redevelopment of the Greater Austin YMCA’s TownLake property as the start of a broader push into Texas, with a 2028 construction start as the goal. The YMCA’s own announcement says it will sell part of its 4.8-acre site on West Cesar Chavez Street to the affiliate MP-Austin and keep the parcel where a new Y will be built. Early plans call for a new YMCA of about 110,000 SF that includes a 10,000 SF early-education center, plus market-rate housing and up to 90 affordable rental units. The current building dates to 1970. Institutional owners with large, aging downtown sites can unlock major land value through partnerships like this one, and I expect more nonprofits and churches near the core to look at the same approach.

Office leasing · Northwest Austin & federal

More detail on 7700 Parmer’s 102,751 SF of new leases, plus a 15-year GSA office lease award in Austin

Hoodline · Austin Business Journal · SAM.gov (via SAM Directory) · Nov 23–24, 2026

Following up on yesterday’s item: Hoodline, citing citybiz, puts Accesso Partners’ four new leases at 7700 Parmer at 102,751 SF. That includes Vaco by Highspring at 27,346 SF and IWG/Regus at 13,448 SF, while an unnamed global professional services firm and an unnamed multinational technology company account for the remaining 61,957 SF. Hoodline also cites Connect CRE and Bisnow reporting that Accesso plans to start a multiphase mixed-use project on the campus’s parking lots in 2027. Separately, a federal award notice posted Nov. 24 shows the General Services Administration awarding an Austin office lease (solicitation 4TX1005) for 23,191 ABOA SF on a 15-year term with 10 years firm, with Braker Metric Business Parks LLC listed as the incumbent. A 10-year firm federal commitment is the kind of credit tenancy that makes an older suburban office asset much easier to finance.

Multifamily · Capital markets

Yardi Matrix: Austin rents are still down 2.5% year over year but have risen five months in a row, and construction lenders are coming back downtown

CRE Daily via Yahoo Finance (Yardi Matrix) · GlobeNewswire (Sunrise Realty Trust) · Nov 2026

According to Yardi Matrix’s November report, as summarized by CRE Daily, Austin’s average asking rent is $1,514, up 0.1% over three months and a fifth straight monthly gain. Rents are still down 2.5% year over year, the weakest of Yardi’s top 30 markets. Stabilized occupancy held at 92.4% after a record 30,719 apartment deliveries in 2025. Only 16 of 44 submarkets showed annual gains, led by Bastrop (+4.5%) and Georgetown-East (+4.3%). Lenders are acting on that bottoming story: Sunrise Realty Trust’s $31 million B-note within a $93 million construction loan for The Grant (232 units at 512 Sabine St.) closed Nov. 23. For my land and investment clients, that suggests Austin multifamily is moving from oversupply toward recovery, with the exurban submarkets leading.

Data centers · Local rules

Data-center check-in: Kyle tabled its ban direction, Bastrop took second reading on new rules, and Austin and Georgetown vote in December

Community Impact · Civic IQ · City of Austin · Nov 17–24, 2026

I can now close one open question. Community Impact reports that on Nov. 17 the Kyle City Council tabled Council Member Claudia Zapata’s item asking staff to bring back an ordinance banning data centers in all zoning districts. That means no ban direction for now, though I’m watching whether it comes back. In Bastrop, a Civic IQ summary of the Nov. 17 meeting says council took second reading on Ordinance 2026-50. The ordinance treats data centers separately from R&D labs, bars them from General Commercial, makes them a conditional use in Industrial, and adds standards for utility demand, water, noise, setbacks and decommissioning. I haven’t seen the official minutes yet. Elsewhere, Austin Council takes up phase one of its data-center rules on Dec. 3, an Austin Energy memo lists 13 inquiries totaling up to 664 MW, and Georgetown Council considers Inner Loop Exchange on Dec. 8 after P&Z recommended denial.

Manufacturing · I-35 & US 79 corridors

Daimler’s Project Hercules: San Marcos says the Caldwell County site is the only Texas option still in the running, with a decision expected before Christmas

Transport Topics · City of San Marcos · Groundbreak · ABJ · Nov 16–24, 2026

Transport Topics reports that Daimler Truck North America is still assessing sites in several states for its largest U.S. plant. According to the City of San Marcos, the 611-acre Caldwell County site in its ETJ, planned for about 2 million SF, is the only Texas location still under consideration. DTNA now expects to start construction in early 2027, a bit later than its original timeline, and still plans to begin operations in 2029. TT also quotes Daimler Truck’s CEO as saying the location will likely be announced before Christmas. San Marcos holds Chapter 380 readings on Dec. 1 and Dec. 15. It is still not a done deal. Also still on my radar: Samsung’s proposed $1.9 billion Northeast Austin fab expansion (Groundbreak) and roughly 160,000 SF of new space planned at TaylorPort (ABJ).
