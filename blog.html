Downtown · Supertall proposal

Project Bolo gets a name: “Austin Lineage,” a 71-story hotel-and-condo tower planned at 400 Congress

Community Impact · Nov 11, 2026 · KVUE via CultureMap Austin · Nov 11, 2026

I’ve been following the parking lot at Congress and Fourth for a couple of weeks, and Wednesday brought real detail. Community Impact reports that city historic review materials filed this month by Lanoha, KPF and House & Robertson Architects describe the Austin Lineage at 400 Congress Ave.: an art deco-inspired, 71-floor, 993-foot tower with ground-floor cafe and bar space, a roughly 30-floor hotel with 221 rooms and a fifth-floor pool deck, and about 30 floors of condominiums with 87 units, topped by a decorative crown. At that height it would be Austin’s second-tallest building behind the 1,025-foot Waterline. KVUE’s report (via CultureMap) adds that the rezoning request covers 0.42 acres at 402 and 408 Congress and seeks DDB850 instead of DDB400 under the Downtown Density Bonus program, which would allow up to 1,200 feet; the developer’s attorney said plans are subject to change, and the rezoning is still under review. For downtown hospitality and luxury condo supply, this is one to watch through the zoning process.

Industrial · Pipeline and sales

“Project Juniper” registers a $67 million, 1-million-SF warehouse on FM 969 in southeast Austin

The American Developer · Nov 11, 2026 · Commercial Property Executive · Nov 10, 2026

A big one surfaced in state filings: The American Developer reports a TDLR registration (TABS2027005567), filed Nov. 10, for a single-story, 1,015,000 SF tilt-up warehouse at 11902 FM 969 for “a confidential client,” with a $67 million estimated cost, a planned Nov. 23 start and December 2027 completion. No design firm or tenant is assigned yet, and the planned start is the registrant’s projection, not proof that work has begun. On the investment side, CPE’s weekly deal sheet says Formation Interests and New York Life bought the fully leased, 154,371 SF industrial property at 7600 Metropolis Drive, Formation’s first Austin investment, with First United Bank & Trust financing. Still ahead: Park 290 Logistics Center’s rezoning goes to Zoning and Platting on Nov. 17.

East Riverside · Mixed use

River Park’s first phase is under construction on East Riverside, with a 4,000-capacity venue due in early 2027

CBS Austin · Nov 11, 2026

CBS Austin checked in on Presidium’s 109-acre River Park. Principal Michael Piano says construction is underway on the 15-acre first phase near Crossing Place and East Riverside Drive, planned for two apartment communities, a hotel, parks and open space alongside United Heritage Hall, the 4,000-capacity venue being developed with AEG Presents and expected to open in early 2027. Per the city, the developer has paid about $4 million in transportation contributions, including $2.4 million toward the Longhorn Bridge project and $1.606 million toward Project Connect. Light rail along Riverside is planned for 2033, so there’s a multi-year gap between the venue opening and rail service, which matters for retail and hospitality planning in the corridor.

Data centers · Grid and policy

ERCOT will ask lawmakers for authority to curtail data centers earlier; Austin’s rules still aimed at Dec. 3

McGregor Brief · Nov 11, 2026 · Spectrum News · Nov 11, 2026 · Austin Politics · TCEQ

The McGregor Brief reports ERCOT CEO Pablo Vegas said Wednesday at the BloombergNEF Summit in Houston that he’ll ask the Legislature for authority to require data centers to cut demand at the first level of a grid emergency, or even before, rather than waiting for level two. Nothing changes until lawmakers act and the PUC writes rules. Spectrum News quotes Gov.-elect Hinojosa saying she doesn’t think she can halt all permitted projects, but will use her power to hold the permits she can. Locally, Austin’s Phase 1 data-center rules are still slated to be posted Nov. 20 for a Dec. 3 Council vote; the staff memo counts 13 Austin Energy inquiries of 20 MW or more, none above 75 MW. Hays County’s Development Regulations hearing is Dec. 1, and I rechecked TCEQ this morning: STEERS is still down for data-center reviews, with the fix still targeted for mid-November.

Suburbs · Land and flex

Robinson family seeks a master developer for 4T Ranch; Driftwood Business Center about half built

Austin Business Journal · Nov 11, 2026 · Community Impact · Nov 11, 2026

Following up on Monday’s 4T Ranch story, ABJ reports the Robinson family plans to hire a master developer for the 1,376-acre former quarry site near Georgetown, which could hold thousands of homes and millions of square feet of commercial space over several decades. Down in the Hill Country, Community Impact profiles Driftwood Business Center at 10020 Darden Hill Road: flex space for lease and sale from 1,750 SF suites to 12,000 SF buildings. Broker Guy Oberg of The Oberg Group at Compass says it’s roughly 50% complete, will total 28 buildings at completion in late 2028, and about 10 buildings are fully leased. It’s a good example of the small-bay flex demand I keep hearing about from local operators.
