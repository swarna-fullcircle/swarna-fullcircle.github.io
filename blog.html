Data centers · Hays County & state policy

Hays County holds off on its new Development Regulations until a Dec. 1 hearing, while data-center bills pile up at the Capitol

Hays County · Nov 10, 2026 · PowerGrab TX · Nov 10, 2026 · TCEQ

I said yesterday I’d report back on Hays County, and the answer is: not yet. At Tuesday’s meeting, Commissioners Court heard Freese & Nichols present the revised final draft and took public comment, then called a public hearing for 11 a.m. on Tuesday, Dec. 1. The county’s own project page says adoption “may follow” that hearing. As a reminder, the draft would tie county incentives for “high-intensity” users (including data centers drawing 20 MW or more) to lower-water cooling and on-site power, and the county’s pause on discretionary approvals for large water users still runs through Dec. 31. At the state level, PowerGrab TX’s Monday recap counts 22 joint authors on Sen. Huffman’s SB 293 to repeal the data-center sales tax exemption (about $3.3 billion in forgone revenue next biennium, per the Comptroller), plus House repeal bills HB 167, HB 408 and HB 851, and a separate Senate package (SB 32–35) covering PUC permits for large computing loads, cost recovery, and county authority over big electricity users. I rechecked TCEQ this morning, too: STEERS is still down for data-center reviews, with the fix still targeted for mid-November.

Downtown · Multifamily delivery

The Travis, a 54-story Rainey Street tower, is complete

Connect CRE · Nov 10, 2026 · Private Wealth Daily · Nov 10, 2026 · KTGY

Genesis Real Estate Group and architect KTGY have delivered The Travis, a 54-story glass residential tower on Rainey Street. Per Connect CRE’s reporting, it has more than 400 units over an eight-story podium with parking, amenities, and a retail corner, and was built with a $173.4 million JPMorgan Chase construction loan. Loan terms weren’t disclosed. For downtown retail and the Rainey district, it’s another big block of rooftops arriving at once; the question I’ll be watching is how fast it leases up alongside the rest of the downtown apartment supply.

Distress · Trustee sales

Travis County’s Dec. 1 foreclosure list grows, with more company borrowers

The American Developer · Nov 10, 2026

The American Developer counted 126 notices of substitute trustee’s sale in Travis County for the Dec. 1 auction as of Nov. 10, and 42 of them name a company rather than a household as borrower, up from 26 of 112 for the Nov. 3 sale. Two filed Nov. 10 stand out: a 32.54-acre tract held by 13201 FM 812 Holdings LLC, and Tract 2 of University Hills Village held by Silverstone Acquisitions LP. Several small-builder lots are on the list too. Notices don’t show loan balances, and many get cancelled or postponed before sale day, so I treat this as an early-warning signal for land and builder stress, not a buying list.

Suburbs · Flex, education, and office

Leander Tech Park signs three leases, ACC moves ahead in Kyle, and CesiumAstro files in Bee Cave

Austin Business Journal · Nov 10, 2026 · TDLR · The American Developer · Nov 10, 2026

Three suburban items from Tuesday. ABJ reports St. John Properties signed Advanced Auto Parts, SafeSplash Swim School and Bahama Buck’s at Leander Tech Park, a 50-acre park planned for 13 buildings and at least 230,000 SF of flex space at full buildout. In Kyle, ABJ says Austin Community College is moving ahead with a large health sciences building at its Hays Campus; the state TDLR filing describes a three-story, 136,300 SF instructional building at 1200 Kohlers Crossing with a $101 million estimated cost and August 2028 completion. And The American Developer reports CesiumAstro registered a $6.6 million finish-out of a 38,770 SF cold-shell building at 16900 Cross Peak Drive in Bee Cave. Also on Tuesday, ABJ reported Portland-based Fortis Construction has opened an Austin office.

Industrial · Pipeline and leasing

Park 290 Logistics Center rezoning heads to commission Nov. 17; Southeast Crossing and Tesla in Buda

The American Developer · Nov 10, 2026 · Hoodline · Austin Business Journal · Partners Real Estate

The Park 290 Logistics Center case (C14-2026-0054) on Decker Lane and the US 290 service road goes to Austin’s Zoning and Platting Commission on Nov. 17, now asking for CH-PDA instead of the LI district listed in October, with staff recommending it. The owner of record is BSREP III Decker Lane LP; acreage isn’t stated. Carrying over from yesterday: Bridge Logistics Properties and Clarion Partners completed the roughly 366,000 SF Southeast Crossing near the airport, and ABJ reports Tesla signed an industrial lease at Buda Commerce Center. For context, Partners has Q3 metro industrial vacancy at 15.5% with 2.21 million SF absorbed.

Georgetown & Taylor · Condensed

Heirloom, 4T Ranch, and Taylor’s Gradiant Technology Park

Austin Business Journal · Nov 9, 2026 · Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo · Nov 5, 2026

Condensing this week’s north-corridor land stories: ABJ reports Heirloom in Georgetown broke ground Nov. 5 with plans for 3,000 residential units and 100 acres of commercial, and the Robinson family is pursuing 4T Ranch, a 1,376-acre mixed-use plan on a former quarry with an in-city MUD under review. In Taylor, Samsung supplier iMarketAmerica is investing more than $50 million in the 212-acre, 2.2 million SF Gradiant Technology Park, with a first phase of at least 325,000 SF that must start by Dec. 31.
