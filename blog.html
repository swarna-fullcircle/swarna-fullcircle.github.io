Multifamily · Affordable

NRP breaks ground on Catalina — 336 affordable units in southeast Austin

REBusinessOnline · Sep 8, 2026

Affordable multifamily is still getting built even as market-rate permits have thinned. Cleveland-based The NRP Group broke ground on Catalina, a 336-unit community in southeast Austin developed with the Housing Authority of Travis County. Twelve three-story buildings will offer one- to four-bedroom homes for households at 30–70% of AMI, with resident services (afterschool care, financial literacy, ESL, first-time homebuyer programs). Tax-credit equity comes from Huntington Community Development Corp.; PIMCO provided construction and permanent financing arranged by Berkadia. First units are expected next fall, with full completion targeted for spring 2028. I’m watching this as a live counterweight to the thinner conventional pipeline.

Multifamily · Investment

Knightvest buys 1,027-unit North Austin multifamily portfolio

REBusinessOnline · Sep 3, 2026 · also Multifamily Business Sep 8

Capital is still rotating into Austin apartments. Newmark arranged the sale of three Northwest Austin / Round Rock communities totaling 1,027 units — The Enclave at La Frontera (411), Lakeside at La Frontera (366), and Legends Lake Creek (250), known as The Silicon Hills Trio — from San Antonio–based LYND to Dallas-based Knightvest Capital. Built 2001–2004, the assets average about 936 SF per unit. Patton Jones and Andrew Dickson represented the seller; Tip Strickland and David Schwarz arranged buyer financing. This is the kind of mid-vintage corridor trade I’m watching as investors re-enter after the supply overhang.

Retail · Southwest Austin

Cohen & Steers and Trademark acquire Oak Hill Plaza

PR Newswire · Aug 31, 2026

Necessity retail at a rebuilt gateway is getting institutional capital. Cohen & Steers’ Real Estate Opportunities Fund, in a JV with Fort Worth–based Trademark Property Company, acquired Oak Hill Plaza in southwest Austin. The center is 92.5% occupied with tenants including Wells Fargo, The Picklr, Pluckers, Dollar Tree, and Autozone, at the US-290 / SH-71 interchange as a major multi-year roadway project nears completion. Cohen & Steers cites a ~60,000 three-mile population, $120,000 median household income, and $688,000 median home value (CoStar, as of mid-June 2026). For me, this is another data point that well-located, income-producing Austin retail still clears for institutional buyers.

Policy · Data centers

Austin City Council advances data center land-use rules

CBS Austin · Aug 28, 2026

Inside the city limits, the zoning conversation just got real. Council unanimously approved a resolution (Aug. 27) directing staff to draft Land Development Code and related regulations for data centers — where they can locate, development standards, and permitting — with binding rules due back by the end of 2026. Members signaled support for the most restrictive workable definition of large / hyperscale facilities, citing water and power. The resolution is not an immediate ban; it starts the code process while staff monitors applications. For MSA land and industrial clients, city-limits underwriting and ETJ / surrounding-corridor sites are diverging themes I’ll keep separating.

Office · Downtown

Hines buys 405 Colorado — fully leased CBD tower at a record PSF

REBusinessOnline · Aug 31, 2026

Trophy office is trading again. An affiliate of Houston-based Hines purchased 405 Colorado, a 206,000-square-foot downtown tower fully leased at closing to tenants including JPMorgan Chase, Bain & Co., and AllianceBernstein. REBusinessOnline, citing the Austin Business Journal, reports Brandywine Realty Trust sold the building for $151 million — about $733 per square foot, the richest downtown office PSF since the 2021 peak. Amenitized, income-backed CBD product is still what I’m watching as capital re-enters selectively.

Hospitality · Downtown

1 Hotel opens inside Waterline — first major tenant in Texas’ tallest tower

CBS Austin · Sep 3, 2026

Waterline’s hospitality stack is live. 1 Hotel Austin is open as the first major tenant in the 1,025-foot downtown tower, bringing restaurants, a rooftop pool, and more than 300 jobs. The brand leaned hard into sustainability (about 4,000 plants, reclaimed materials, filtered water in every room). For CRE, this is the next chapter after delivery: can office and luxury residential lease into a selective CBD while hospitality and tourism demand keep filling the mixed-use stack?

Capital markets · Leander

Walker & Dunlop arranges $46.5M bridge refinance for River Junction

Walker & Dunlop · also REBusinessOnline syndication

North-corridor debt is still clearing for newer Class A product. Walker & Dunlop arranged a $46.5 million floating-rate, interest-only bridge loan through Bridge Investment Group to refinance River Junction — a 329-unit, 2024-completed community in Leander with 15 buildings (apartments plus 17 townhomes). Patrick Short and Clay Colvill arranged the loan. I’m reading this as another signal that lenders will back employment-driven suburban lease-up when the asset is amenitized and the corridor thesis (semiconductor, tech, data-center adjacency) still holds.

Industrial · Georgetown

Aalo Atomics plans 200,000 SF manufacturing build-out in Georgetown

Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo Finance · Sep 2, 2026

I’m still watching the north corridor. Austin-based Aalo Atomics finalized plans for a more than 200,000-square-foot production and manufacturing facility in Georgetown — about 40 miles north of its East Ben White headquarters — tied to modular nuclear plants serving power-hungry data centers. A TDLR filing points to roughly $25 million to renovate a one-story warehouse off I-35 (lab, machine shop, production floor), with work expected to start Nov. 15 and wrap by January 2028.
