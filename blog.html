Data centers / state permitting · Statewide, including Bastrop and Caldwell counties

TCEQ’s Oct. 19 update to the governor came and went without a public copy — and a new watchdog report says permitting never fully stopped, including at two Austin-area campuses

Inside Climate News · Oct 19, 2026 · Public Citizen · Oct 2026 · Texas Construction Hub · Oct 19, 2026 · Austin Politics · Oct 18, 2026

Monday was TCEQ’s deadline to tell Gov. Abbott how it is complying with his Sept. 21 directive to halt data-center permits. When I checked TCEQ’s newsroom this morning, its latest release was still from Oct. 7, and I haven’t found a public copy of the update, so I’m not going to guess at what it says. Texas Construction Hub’s Oct. 19 explainer makes the key point for owners and contractors: Oct. 19 was a reporting deadline, not an automatic restart of approvals. Meanwhile, Inside Climate News reports that FracTracker Alliance and Public Citizen identified 35 TCEQ permitting actions on data-center projects in the weeks after the directive — 14 permits fast-tracked or assigned to engineers, stormwater coverage approved for 11, emissions estimates certified for seven, and public notice issued for three. Closest to home, the groups list stormwater approvals for Caldwell Valley Technology Park in Caldwell County and EdgeConneX’s Cedar Creek campus in Bastrop County. Abbott’s office and TCEQ hadn’t responded to Inside Climate News when it published. On the city side, Austin Politics notes the city manager’s 60-day update on Austin’s own data-center rules falls in late October, with staff aiming for an initial phase by year-end that could let Council bar the largest sites as early as December. For land sellers and industrial owners east of Austin, the practical read is that permit status now has to be checked project by project.

Industrial / spec development · The Row, Southeast Austin near the airport

Seefried Industrial Properties plans a 200,000-plus-square-foot warehouse at The Row near Austin-Bergstrom, with the tenant still unnamed

Austin Business Journal · Oct 19, 2026 · Groundbreak project file · Oct 19, 2026

A big industrial filing at one of Southeast Austin’s marquee sites. ABJ reports that Seefried Industrial Properties is developing a warehouse of more than 200,000 square feet at The Row near the airport, with public filings showing it could go under construction next year; ABJ says the tenant remains unknown. Groundbreak’s project file, which labels the building as Amazon-linked, lists Seefried as owner and Presidium as developer, with 200,000 square feet on 52.7 acres, a $60 million construction cost, a Jan. 11, 2027 start and an Oct. 11, 2027 completion. Those dates and that cost match the “Project Phantom” warehouse filing near the airport I wrote about yesterday, though the square footage reported differs and I haven’t seen a source confirm they’re the same building, so I’m treating that as an open question. Either way, it’s another sign that developers still see room for big-box product near the airport even as the metro works through industrial oversupply.

Land use / comprehensive plan · Georgetown, Williamson County

Tonight in Georgetown: Planning & Zoning takes up land-use changes on more than 4,200 acres along Ronald Reagan Boulevard and SH 195

City of Georgetown P&Z agenda via My Texas Record · Oct 20, 2026

This one hits close to my Ronald Reagan Boulevard office. Georgetown’s Planning and Zoning Commission meets at 6 p.m. tonight at 510 W. 9th St. The biggest item is a 2030 Comprehensive Plan amendment covering three areas: about 1,528 acres north of Ronald W. Reagan Boulevard and west of SH 195 moving from “Mining” to “Mixed Density Neighborhood” and from “Rural Residential” to “Neighborhood”; about 125 acres near Rattlesnake Road moving from “Mining” to “Mixed Density Neighborhood”; and about 2,624 acres east of SH 195 and north of Sun City Boulevard moving from “Employment Center” to “Mixed Density Neighborhood” and from “Mining” to “Regional Center” and “Mixed Density Neighborhood.” Also on the agenda: rezoning 20.579 acres at 31801 Ronald W. Reagan Boulevard to Public Facility for the Northlands Water Reclamation Facility, a spring-buffer variance for the Ronald Reagan Boulevard widening at the Berry Creek bridge, a third amendment to the Parmer Ranch development agreement, and rezoning 1001 S. College St. to Mixed-Use Downtown for a real estate office. These are commission hearings ahead of Council decisions, so nothing here is final tonight — but future-land-use maps like this shape where retail, services and employment land end up in north Williamson County.

Infrastructure / airport expansion · Austin-Bergstrom International Airport

AUS breaks ground on a campus-wide utility project that sets up its next phase of expansion

Austin-Bergstrom International Airport · Oct 19, 2026

The airport celebrated the groundbreaking of its Utilities Infrastructure Campus-Wide project on Oct. 19 — new aircraft-rated stormwater and water-quality trunk lines and ponds in the southwest part of the airport, redundant medium-voltage power systems, telecommunications duct banks, and upgrades to storm drainage, electrical distribution and hydronic piping. Sundt Archer Western is prime contractor and HDR is the designer; construction started this fall and is expected to finish in 2029. Funding comes from airport reserves and revenues, future revenue bonds and FAA grants, including about $10.1 million from the Airport Infrastructure Grants program and $6.1 million from the Airport Improvement Program. Mayor Kirk Watson framed it as groundwork for 30 to 35 million annual passengers once the Journey With AUS expansion is complete. Read alongside the warehouse filings near the airport, it’s a reminder of how much long-term capital is converging on Southeast Austin.

Land & master-planned communities · Northeast Austin and Bastrop County

Colony Park’s developers go looking for homebuilders, and Silverleaf registers two more phases in Bastrop County

Austin Business Journal · Oct 19, 2026 · Hoodline, citing the Statesman · Oct 17, 2026 · The American Developer · Oct 15 & 19, 2026

Two eastern land stories. ABJ reports that the developers behind Mueller are nearing horizontal work at Colony Park, the city-owned 208 acres at 7900 Loyola Lane, and that a timeline is coming into focus as they look for homebuilders; development work is planned to start next year. The state filing I flagged last week puts Phase 1 roads and utilities at $45 million with a March 1, 2027 start and Sept. 1, 2028 completion. Per the Statesman’s reporting, summarized by Hoodline, zoning allows up to 3,000 homes and 960,000 square feet of commercial and civic space, at least 20% of homes must be affordable, and Central Health’s $37 million, 34,000-square-foot clinic is already under construction for a summer 2027 opening. Farther east, The American Developer reports Atlantis WKA Bastrop LLC registered a $10 million TDLR filing on Oct. 9 for Silverleaf Phases 2 and 9 — a 2,118,382-square-foot scope that, at under $5 per square foot, points to site or common-area work rather than a single building. As the publication notes, that’s a state accessibility filing, not a county building permit.

Retail / investment sales · Oak Hill, Southwest Austin

Cohen & Steers and Trademark buy the 115,512-square-foot Oak Hill Plaza at the “Y,” with a revitalization plan now that the Oak Hill Parkway rebuild is substantially complete

Groundbreak · Oct 18, 2026

A good weekend retail read for anyone watching Southwest Austin. Groundbreak reports that a joint venture of Cohen & Steers Real Estate Opportunities Fund and Trademark Property Company acquired Oak Hill Plaza, a 115,512-square-foot center on 11.28 acres at 7101 State Highway 71 W — the “Oak Hill Y” where US 290 West meets SH 71 — on Aug. 21. The center was 92.5% occupied at acquisition, with tenants including Wells Fargo, The Picklr, Pluckers, Dollar Tree, AutoZone, Jim’s Restaurant and Golden Chick. Partners Capital (Partners Real Estate) sold, Newmark brokered, and terms weren’t disclosed; Trademark will manage, lease and lead a revitalization. The deal follows substantial completion of TxDOT’s $674 million Oak Hill Parkway project, and it’s Cohen & Steers’ second Austin-area shopping center buy this year after 1890 Ranch in Cedar Park. To me, that’s institutional capital betting on well-located, older suburban retail once the road work is behind it.

Office / tenant build-out · The Domain, North Austin

A 38,380-square-foot “Take2 ATX” office build-out got underway Monday at Domain 7

Groundbreak · Oct 19, 2026

A concrete office-leasing signal in North Austin. Groundbreak reports that Take2 ATX, a 38,380-square-foot tenant build-out inside Domain 7 — a 221,973-square-foot Class A building at 11501 Alterra Parkway — is registered with TDLR at an estimated $3.5 million (about $91 per square foot), with construction starting Oct. 19 and completion expected Feb. 5, 2027. The scope covers demolition, new partitions, ceilings, doors, finishes and MEP updates. Groundbreak notes the project name and scope indicate a build-out for Take-Two Interactive; the filing itself lists Domain Junction 7 LLC as owner of record. Build-outs of this size are a reminder that The Domain keeps drawing tech tenants even with metro office vacancy elevated.
