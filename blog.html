Office · Downtown

Hines buys 405 Colorado — fully leased CBD tower

REBusinessOnline · Aug 31, 2026

Trophy office is trading again. An affiliate of Houston-based Hines purchased 405 Colorado, a 206,000-square-foot downtown tower that was fully leased at closing to tenants including JPMorgan Chase, Bain & Co., and AllianceBernstein. REBusinessOnline, citing the Austin Business Journal, reports Brandywine Realty Trust sold the building for $151 million. Amenities include a 14th-floor sky lounge with conference rooms and a café — the kind of amenitized, income-backed CBD asset I’m watching as capital selectively re-enters Austin office.

Industrial · Georgetown

Aalo Atomics plans 200,000 SF manufacturing build-out in Georgetown

Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo Finance · Sep 2, 2026

I’m still watching the north corridor. Austin-based Aalo Atomics finalized plans for a more than 200,000-square-foot production and manufacturing facility in Georgetown — about 40 miles north of its East Ben White headquarters — tied to modular nuclear plants serving power-hungry data centers. A TDLR filing points to roughly $25 million to renovate a one-story warehouse off I-35 (lab, machine shop, production floor), with work expected to start Nov. 15 and wrap by January 2028.

Multifamily · Market

Matthews: Austin multifamily vacancy improves sharply in Q2

Matthews · Q2 2026

The apartment supply story is turning. Matthews reports Austin multifamily vacancy fell 289 basis points year-over-year to 12.29% in Q2 2026 — off a 15.78% peak in Q3 2024 — while effective rent was $1,414 (−3.88% YoY, roughly half the decline of a year earlier). Completions plunged and the under-construction pipeline shrank to 4.55% of inventory from a 19.67% peak. Q2 investment volume nearly tripled YoY to $557.5M. For CRE clients, this is the clearest supply-driven recovery signal I’ve seen in our multifamily stack this cycle.

Mixed-use · Downtown

Waterline completes: Texas’ tallest tower delivers downtown

REBusinessOnline · Aug 31, 2026

Lincoln Property Co. and Kairoi Residential have completed Waterline at 98 Red River St. — 74 stories and 1,025 feet, now the tallest building in Texas. The 2.7 million SF mixed-use stack includes about 350 apartments (Bosque Residences), roughly 700,000 SF of office, a ~251-room 1 Hotels property, and 24,000 SF of ground-floor retail. Construction finished about two months early. I’m watching how this office and luxury residential supply leases into a still-selective CBD.

Leasing · Industrial

Kent Cos. takes 39,894 SF at Tuscany X Crossing

REBusinessOnline via Faribai · Aug 28, 2026

Smaller industrial deals still matter for absorption. Michigan-based Kent Cos., a specialty concrete provider, signed a 39,894 SF lease to relocate and expand at Tuscany X Crossing Business Park — a 329,627 SF Brookfield Properties development about two miles north of I-35 in northeast Austin. Bradford’s Luke Clardy represented the tenant; Aquila’s Blake Patterson represented the landlord. Another incremental north/northeast industrial win while big-box vacancy stays elevated.

Retail · Investment

Partners Capital sells Oak Hill Plaza along SH 71

Bisnow · Sep 1, 2026

Retail capital is still active here. Partners Capital sold the 116,000 SF Oak Hill Plaza at 7101 State Highway 71 to a Trademark Property / Cohen & Steers joint venture. Price wasn’t disclosed; TCAD appraisal is about $19.4M. The center is 92.5% leased (Picklr, Dollar Tree, AutoZone, Wells Fargo among tenants). Tight retail fundamentals continue to support investor appetite across the MSA.

Data centers · Adaptive reuse

SoftBank affiliate details plans for former 3M Austin campus

Austin American-Statesman

I’m still tracking the former 3M campus in northwest Austin. An SEC filing from SE Cosmos LLC (SB Energy / SoftBank-linked) outlines converting the ~1.1M SF office campus into an R&D / tech center with substantial IT load — even as Austin leaders consider limits on large and hyperscale data centers inside city limits. Adaptive reuse of major campuses remains a live CRE theme for me across the MSA.
