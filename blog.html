Williamson & Hays · Data-center policy

Ahead of Tuesday: what the Inner Loop Exchange developers are promising Georgetown, and Kyle’s ban push

Connect CRE · Texas Construction Hub · Nov 13, 2026 · KVUE · Nov 12, 2026

We’re two days from the Nov. 17 data-center night, and the Georgetown case has more detail now. Connect CRE, citing ABJ, reports that Oakwell Capital Partners and Cawley Partners propose a 68 MW, 145,000 SF data center building. The zoning change would allow up to 290,000 SF for future growth, with capital investment approaching $1 billion. To address power concerns, the developers point to Oncor building a large substation at the site, and they say water use will go beyond code requirements. Oakwell’s Phillip Niels told the outlet the project “sets in place real enforceable guardrails around sound and water and light.” Texas Construction Hub explains why the request is a big one: the site’s current commercial zoning caps a data center at 5,000 SF, so going to 290,000 SF is a 58-fold increase, and the application has been reviewed as both a zoning amendment and a PUD. Planning and Zoning holds its hearing at 6 p.m. Tuesday on the roughly 28 acres near Northeast Inner Loop and East University Avenue. Also on Tuesday, Kyle’s council takes up Council Member Claudia Zapata’s push to prohibit data centers in every zoning district (KVUE). If you own land along the corridor, these two outcomes are worth watching side by side.

Land use · West Austin

Muny vote slides to Dec. 3, and the UT Brackenridge land could carry about 4.9 million SF

Austin Politics · Nov 14, 2026

This is one of the biggest entitlement stories in central Austin. Austin Politics reports that Council won’t vote Nov. 19 on the agreement with UT to take ownership of Lions Municipal Golf Course; the item now carries a Dec. 3 date. Staff slides for Tuesday’s 9 a.m. work session compare the proposed rules for the roughly 190 acres UT could develop for non-university uses with the 1989 Brackenridge agreement. Total floor area would go from about 1.7 million SF to about 4.9 million SF (0.6 FAR across 190 acres), with no apartment density cap, 75% building coverage per site, and heights of 65 feet along and north of Lake Austin Boulevard and 105 feet south of it. The city would get permanent ownership of the 141-acre course, and a new tax increment zone would let UT’s development pay for infrastructure. The Mueller lease with UT would also be amended. If Council approves on Dec. 3, staff list the regulating plan, street vacations and the tax zone’s finance plan for spring 2027.

Industrial · Tech filings

A $10M warehouse renovation on Burleson Road, and Dell files a second Round Rock server upgrade in a week

The American Developer · Nov 14, 2026

Two state filings caught my eye Friday. A $10 million renovation of a 124,313 SF warehouse at 7051 Burleson Road in Southeast Austin was registered with TDLR on Nov. 13. The owner is CTX Burleson Industrial LP, the scope covers warehouse, industrial and office tenant improvements with phased handovers, and work runs from Jan. 8 to June 10, 2027. The record calls the project “Kendra Scott Warehouse,” but the tenant field says “Not Assigned,” and The American Developer cautions to treat the name as a label, not a lease announcement. Up in Round Rock, Dell USA LP registered an $8.0 million, 3,205 SF server and MEP renovation of Building 3 at 701 E. Parmer Lane, with a planned Dec. 17 start. Together with last week’s $12 million Building 2 lab filing, that’s $20 million of compute-related work at one campus in seven days.

Retail · Medical office

Rooms To Go heads to Leander’s Northline, Woodside Health buys West Lake Hills medical office, and retail rents keep climbing

Hoodline (citing Community Impact) · Nov 12–14, 2026 · Commercial Property Executive · Nov 10, 2026

Retail and medical office keep showing up in the suburbs. Hoodline, citing Community Impact, reports that Rooms To Go plans a roughly 49,182 SF showroom in the Northline development at 10021 183A Toll Frontage Road in Leander. Construction is slated for January 2027 with completion expected by December 2027, and a 52,460 SF store is also underway in San Marcos. Commercial Property Executive reports that Woodside Health bought The Hills Medical Portfolio, two medical office buildings totaling 55,000 SF at 4613 and 4701 Bee Caves Road in West Lake Hills. Gabriel’s Court, the 45,658 SF office, medical and retail property near West 29th and North Lamar, also traded this week to Realty Capital Partners and The Town Lake Company. On Austin’s proposed commercial rent buy-down for local and legacy businesses, Hoodline adds Partners Real Estate’s Q3 numbers: average retail asking rent of $26.87/SF, up 2.9% year over year, and vacancy of 3.7%.

Earlier this week · Condensed

Springdale Green’s record C-PACE, Titan in Hutto, a Teri Road hotel, Project Juniper and Dog’s Head

Commercial Observer · Austin Business Journal · The American Developer · Hoodline · Community Impact · Nov 11–13, 2026

Jay Paul Company’s $227.5 million C-PACE loan from Nuveen Green Capital on the 881,427 SF Springdale Green campus is the largest C-PACE deal in Texas history (Commercial Observer). Titan’s “Hutto 10 Spec” is about 525,000 SF at 2234 Limmer Loop, $33 million, with a Jan. 8, 2027 start (ABJ, The American Developer). Austin permitted a five-story, 95-unit hotel at 1620 Teri Rd (The American Developer). Project Juniper, the 1,015,000 SF, $67 million warehouse at 11902 FM 969, still has no named client ahead of its Nov. 23 filed start (Hoodline). Council locked in Dog’s Head TIRZ shares for housing and parks while a coalition petitions to end the Endeavor deal (Community Impact).
