Austin CRE Market Update — July 2026

ECR · Aug 10, 2026

I’m watching capital come back into the market with real conviction. Hines paid $151M ($733/SF) for fully leased 405 Colorado — the strongest downtown office PSF since 2021. Tesla leased 682,000 SF at Austin Hills Commerce Center, the region’s largest speculative industrial box. Austin startups raised about $5B in H1 2026, and office absorption is holding while footprints get smaller.

Transparent.city · 2026

New permits for offices, large apartments, and retail have fallen sharply versus 2025. Through mid-August 2026 there was only one office building permit (vs. 17 in all of 2025). A quieter pipeline matters for how I think about future supply across the MSA.

Multifamily

Austin multifamily stabilizes as construction cools

CRE Daily · citing Colliers Q2 2026

Occupancy rose 70 bps from Q1 even as inventory growth slowed. Q2 absorption outpaced new supply. Rents are still soft near-term, but Colliers expects a gradual recovery into 2027 as deliveries ease — a useful signal for the broader metro.
