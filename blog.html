Northeast Austin · Semiconductors · Industrial

Samsung proposes a $1.9 billion expansion of its Northeast Austin fab, with operations targeted for 2030

Groundbreak · Seoul Economic Daily · Nov 15–20, 2026

The biggest new item on my list this morning is in Northeast Austin. Groundbreak reports that Samsung Austin Semiconductor has proposed a $1.9 billion expansion of its fab at 12100 Samsung Blvd., with the expanded lines targeted to begin operating in 2030. According to Groundbreak and the Seoul Economic Daily, the added capacity would make STMicroelectronics microcontrollers on an 18nm FD-SOI process for SpaceX’s Starlink satellites. Groundbreak notes that Samsung says it cannot confirm individual customer contracts and SpaceX has not commented, so I treat the Starlink link as reported, not confirmed. The Austin fab currently runs mature nodes from 65nm to 14nm. Groundbreak says the project falls within Samsung’s broader $37 billion Texas investment across Austin and Taylor, and that it is separate from the Tesla chip agreement tied to Taylor. For my clients, a reinvestment of this size at an existing campus supports supplier, flex-industrial and workforce housing demand along the US 290 East and SH 130 corridors.

Industrial · Q3 market data

Industrial leasing hits 9.77M SF through Q3 while vacancy stays near 19%, and Project Juniper is scheduled to start today

Business News Austin · Austin Business Journal · Community Impact · Groundbreak · Nov 17–22, 2026

Business News Austin compared third-quarter reports from five brokerages. CBRE counts 9.77 million SF of industrial leasing year to date, more than all of 2024 or 2025, and 1.22 million SF of Q3 net absorption. Vacancy is still high: Lee & Associates puts it at 19.26%, Savills at 16.1% and Matthews at 14.0%, and each firm tracks a different set of buildings. Cushman & Wakefield says Georgetown accounts for about 2.4 million SF of leasing this year, roughly 28% of its regional total. Large deals named in the reports include Molto Flex at 310,366 SF in Georgetown and Titan AI Prefab Solutions at 196,523 SF in Hutto. New supply keeps coming. Project Juniper’s TDLR filing (Community Impact) lists a start date of today, Nov. 23, for the $67 million, roughly 1 million SF warehouse at 11902 FM 969, and ABJ ties the project to Link Logistics, with filings pointing to completion late next year. I haven’t seen confirmation that work has actually started. Groundbreak also reports Titan’s 526,400 SF speculative cross-dock building in Hutto, with groundbreaking planned for Q4 2026. For tenants, this is still a good market to negotiate in, especially for new big-box space in the Southeast and East submarkets.

Data centers · Local & state rules

Data-center rules check-in: Georgetown on Dec. 8, Austin on Dec. 3, and the Kyle and Bastrop outcomes still not confirmed

Community Impact · City of Austin · KVUE · City of Bastrop · CBS Austin · KCBD · Nov 6–20, 2026

Georgetown City Council takes up the Inner Loop Exchange rezoning (about 28 acres, up to 290,000 SF and 68 MW) on Dec. 8. P&Z unanimously recommended denial, so approval needs six of seven votes (Community Impact). Austin’s first-phase package goes to Council Dec. 3; it defines “data center” and adds business registration rules, with land-use rules expected by May 2027 (City of Austin). In Kyle, KVUE reports the Nov. 17 item was only about directing staff to bring back a ban ordinance; any ban would need a later formal vote, and I haven’t seen an outcome published. Bastrop put the second reading of its data-center ordinance on the Nov. 17 consent agenda, but no minutes have been posted, so I can’t confirm it passed. Near Cedar Creek, the Pacifico gas-plant permit is still pending at TCEQ, and CBS Austin reports Anthropic is in talks for the campus but has not signed a lease. At the state level, Gov.-elect Hinojosa is calling for a pause on new permits, and Sen. Perry’s SB 338 includes a 50% cap on local tax abatements (KCBD). Where data centers will be allowed, and on what terms, is being decided right now.

Suburban retail & office · Leander, Georgetown, Hill Country

Leander Tech Park lands its first three tenants, and builders say Class A office demand is moving to Round Rock and Cedar Park

Community Impact · Austin Business Journal · Nov 17–20, 2026

Close to my Leander office, Community Impact reports that St. John Properties has signed the first three tenants at Leander Tech Park, a 50-acre business community at Hwy. 29 and Ronald Reagan Boulevard. Advance Auto Parts will build a 7,000 SF store, and SafeSplash Swim School and Bahama Buck’s will open on Kauffman Loop, all in 2027. St. John is building the first phase on spec: two buildings of 51,120 and 38,120 SF plus about 20,000 SF of inline retail. TDLR filings in Community Impact’s weekly permit list include a $4.5 million Sprouts on East Hwy. 290 in Dripping Springs and a $1.6 million Chipotle at 460 Kauffman Loop in Georgetown. In an ABJ Table of Experts piece, W.E. O’Neil’s preconstruction team says several developers are reviving shelved office projects, often smaller or redesigned, and that much of this activity is in suburbs such as Round Rock and Cedar Park rather than downtown. That matches what I’m hearing from owner-users on the north side.

Central Austin · Land & towers

Muny and Brackenridge go to Council Dec. 3, UT studies housing at Dobie and Brackenridge, and Manifold still plans a 47-story office tower

KUT · Austin Business Journal · Groundbreak · Nov 18–22, 2026

KUT reports that UT has agreed to give Lions Municipal Golf Course to the city in exchange for an undisclosed anonymous donation to the university. UT would keep the nearly 200-acre Brackenridge tract around it, and the city plans to add environmental rules on setbacks and density to the updated development agreement. Council is scheduled to decide Dec. 3, and the UT System Board of Regents must also approve. Separately, ABJ reports that UT is considering future housing plans at the Brackenridge and Dobie sites. Downtown, ABJ says Manifold Development is still planning Block 16 on Trinity Street, a 47-story office tower of about 743,175 SF and 723 feet tall. Groundbreak notes that White Lodging’s 258-room Hotel Trinity has opened at 311 E. Fifth St., and that Council has initiated historic landmark zoning for the John Henry Faulk Building at 800 Guadalupe St. Prime central land continues to change hands slowly and through public processes.

Infrastructure & manufacturing · Follow-ups

The $412.2M I-35 rebuild is formally awarded, with work set for Feb. 2027 to Feb. 2032, and Daimler’s San Marcos decision is still pending

Texas Construction Hub · City of San Marcos · Austin Business Journal · Nov 19–22, 2026

Texas Construction Hub confirms that on Nov. 20 the Texas Transportation Commission awarded Austin Bridge & Road Services the $412.2 million contract for I-35 from US 290 East to MLK Jr. Boulevard. The contract drew six bids. TDLR records list a construction start of Feb. 1, 2027 and completion on Feb. 17, 2032, including work at Manor Road, 32nd Street and 38 1/2 Street. Owners along that stretch should plan for about five years of construction. On Project Hercules, the City of San Marcos still describes Daimler Truck North America’s proposed plant as one of several sites under consideration and the only one in Texas, with a site decision expected by year-end. The Chapter 380 agreement’s first reading is Dec. 1 and the second is Dec. 15. No investment figure has been published, and this is not a done deal.
