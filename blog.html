Industrial · Georgetown

Aalo Atomics plans 200,000 SF manufacturing build-out in Georgetown

Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo Finance · Sep 2, 2026

I’m watching north-corridor industrial again this week. Austin-based Aalo Atomics finalized plans for a more than 200,000-square-foot production and manufacturing facility in Georgetown — about 40 miles north of its East Ben White headquarters — tied to modular nuclear plants serving power-hungry data centers. A TDLR filing points to roughly $25 million to renovate a one-story warehouse off I-35 (lab, machine shop, production floor), with work expected to start Nov. 15 and wrap by January 2028.

Mixed-use · Downtown

Waterline completes: Texas’ tallest tower delivers downtown

REBusinessOnline · Aug 31, 2026

Lincoln Property Co. and Kairoi Residential have completed Waterline at 98 Red River St. — 74 stories and 1,025 feet, now the tallest building in Texas. The 2.7 million SF mixed-use stack includes about 350 apartments (Bosque Residences), roughly 700,000 SF of office, a ~251-room 1 Hotels property, and 24,000 SF of ground-floor retail. Construction finished about two months early. I’m watching how this office and luxury residential supply leases into a still-selective CBD.

Retail · Investment

Partners Capital sells Oak Hill Plaza along SH 71

Bisnow · Sep 1, 2026

Retail capital is still active here. Partners Capital sold the 116,000 SF Oak Hill Plaza at 7101 State Highway 71 to a Trademark Property / Cohen & Steers joint venture. Price wasn’t disclosed; TCAD appraisal is about $19.4M. The center is 92.5% leased (Picklr, Dollar Tree, AutoZone, Wells Fargo among tenants). Matthews recently put Q2 retail absorption at 312K SF with metro vacancy around 3.4% — tight fundamentals that help explain investor appetite.

Industrial · Spec pipeline

Green Point plans 754K SF Georgetown spec warehouse

Connect CRE · Aug 24, 2026

Georgetown continues to lead big-box industrial. Affiliates of Dallas-based Green Point Property plan a 754,000 SF speculative building at GTX Logistics Park (1000 FM 972) — positioned as the largest speculative industrial box in the Austin metro. Connect CRE cites an estimated $42M concrete tilt-wall shell, with construction expected to start in October and complete by November 2027. The park is still marketing roughly 2.6M SF on remaining acreage.

Industrial · Last-mile

Amazon’s Georgetown sortation facility moves forward

Hello Georgetown · Jul 9, 2026

A July TDLR filing shows Amazon.com Services LLC planning a 248,687 SF Sub Same Day sortation warehouse at 104 Wittera Way in Titan Development’s Gateway35 park — roughly a $48M project, with groundbreaking targeted for late August 2026 and completion around July 2027. If finished, it would be Amazon’s fourth warehouse in greater Austin (joining Round Rock and Pflugerville). Another demand signal on the I-35 north industrial corridor.

Leasing · Industrial

Tesla’s Mustang Ridge lease cements industrial footprint

The Real Deal · Jul 29, 2026

Tesla leased a 538,720 SF speculative industrial building in Mustang Ridge (Clay Development’s Mustang Ridge Distribution Center I at 6925 FM 1327), bringing its leased Austin-area industrial holdings to about 3.4M SF after the earlier 682K SF Austin Hills Commerce Center deal near Gigafactory Texas. Owned space is still far larger — but as a tenant, Tesla is now one of the metro’s largest industrial users.

Data centers · Adaptive reuse

SoftBank affiliate details plans for former 3M Austin campus

Austin American-Statesman

I’m still tracking the former 3M campus in northwest Austin. An SEC filing from SE Cosmos LLC (SB Energy / SoftBank-linked) outlines converting the ~1.1M SF office campus into an R&D / tech center with substantial IT load — even as Austin leaders consider limits on large and hyperscale data centers inside city limits. Adaptive reuse of major campuses remains a live CRE theme for me across the MSA.
