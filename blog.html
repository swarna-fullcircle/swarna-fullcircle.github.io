Data centers / land use · Hutto, Taylor & City of Austin

Hutto P&Z hears the 97.9-acre data-center campus replat tonight, as Skybox’s next Hutto phase waits on power and Taylor’s parkland site draws scrutiny

Texas Construction Hub · Nov 2, 2026 · City of Hutto P&Z agenda · Nov 3, 2026 meeting · Taylor Press · CBS Austin · Nov 2, 2026 · City of Austin memo · Oct 28, 2026

Tonight, Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7 p.m., Hutto’s Planning and Zoning Commission holds a public hearing on the Hutto Data Center Campus Subdivision One replat: 97.866 acres north and east of CR 132, arranged into six commercial lots. Texas Construction Hub notes the notice doesn’t name a developer, operator, capacity or contractor, and that a replat alone doesn’t confirm a construction start. I’ll report the outcome tomorrow. For context, the Taylor Press reports Hutto Council rezoned a 140-acre Megasite parcel owned by the Hutto EDC for Skybox Datacenters and partner Prologis, now called Hutto Data Center Campus East, with conditions including closed-loop cooling. Skybox’s chief development officer told the city that securing ERCOT power “may take another two years.” Skybox says its first Hutto project, the 600-MW PowerCampus Austin, is fully leased. In Taylor, CBS Austin’s “The Receipts” traces how land conveyed in 1999 for future park use was sold by Taylor’s EDC in April 2025 for $10 million to a data-center developer, and notes the Texas attorney general is investigating. In Austin, the Oct. 28 memo still points to a Dec. 3 Council vote on Phase 1 data-center rules. My read: power and title diligence are now as central to these sites as zoning.

Multifamily · Domain, Leander & Georgetown

Weidner is revealed as Solaris’ buyer, and Benefit Street refinances Greystar-run communities in Leander and Georgetown

Austin Business Journal · Nov 2, 2026 · CRE Daily · Nov 2, 2026 · Commercial Property Executive · Nov 2, 2026

ABJ reports Weidner Apartment Homes bought Solaris, the Domain tower sold by a Brandywine Realty Trust joint venture, and frames the deal as a sign Austin’s multifamily market could slowly be recovering. Per CPE, the 341-unit property sold for $136 million at 90.3% occupancy. CRE Daily reports Benefit Street Partners provided $117.3 million of floating-rate, interest-only refinancing, arranged by Walker & Dunlop, on a 960-unit portfolio that includes Aven Ridge (312 units, Leander) and Austin Avenue (312 units, Georgetown). The same newsletter says Austin delivered 6,388 units in the first half of 2026, down 63% from a year earlier. My read: with deliveries falling that sharply, lenders and buyers are leaning back in on newer suburban product.

Mixed-use & manufacturing · Georgetown & Round Rock

Southwestern University 560 is under construction in Georgetown, and Curtiss-Wright plans a $30 million Round Rock expansion

citybiz · Nov 2, 2026 · Austin Business Journal · Nov 2, 2026 · Community Impact · Oct 29, 2026

Southwestern University and the City of Georgetown have started construction on Southwestern University 560, beginning with a 75,000-square-foot city Customer Service Center for 300-plus employees, with about 5,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, expected to open mid-2028. The 67-acre first phase plans 110,000 SF of office and research, 45,000 SF of retail, 150 apartments, a 110,000-SF hotel and conference center and a 38,000-SF entertainment venue. Total cost wasn’t disclosed. In Round Rock, ABJ reports aerospace and defense supplier Curtiss-Wright plans a $30 million expansion. And Georgetown’s Heirloom (620 acres, 3,600 units) still breaks ground Thursday, Nov. 5. My read: a civic anchor tenant is a smart way to de-risk the first phase of a mixed-use district, and I’ll be watching who follows.

Office & net lease · Market-wide, Northwest Austin & Kyle

Lucid opens at Stonebridge Plaza with a second Austin location set for Las Cimas, while office vacancy holds at 25%

Commercial Property Executive · Nov 2, 2026 · Avison Young · Q3 2026

CPE reports Lucid Private Offices opened about 22,000 square feet covering the full ninth floor of Intercontinental’s Stonebridge Plaza II at 9600 N. MoPac, with 83 private offices and 12 team suites. A second, 23,029-square-foot location is set to open in April 2027 at 805 Las Cimas Parkway, and Lucid plans two more Austin locations by the end of 2027. Citing CoworkingCafe, CPE puts Austin at 122 coworking spaces totaling 2.2 million SF. Big picture: Avison Young counts 289,913 SF of positive Q3 absorption, but vacancy is still 25.0% after Waterline delivered, and only 195,707 SF is under construction. On the net-lease side, CPE’s deal sheet says Fortec bought the 17,900-SF Plum Creek KinderCare at 6022 McNaughton in Kyle, on a 15-year absolute net lease.

City Council · Nov. 5 agenda

Thursday’s Council agenda: a 545-unit North Lamar rezoning, up to $695 million in Austin Energy bonds, and a storage project staff oppose

Austin Politics · Oct 29, 2026 · City of Austin · Nov 5, 2026 meeting page

Council meets Thursday, Nov. 5. Per Austin Politics, items 31 and 32 would rezone 8605–8613 North Lamar Blvd. and 604–706 Beaver Street to DB90 for about 545 apartments and a small retail plaza, with staff and the Planning Commission recommending approval. Item 3 would authorize up to $695 million in Austin Energy revenue bonds. Item 33 would rezone 2.89 acres at 3601 Davis Lane for a mini-warehouse; staff recommend denial, though Zoning and Platting voted 8–1 to approve it with conditions. The agenda is a draft and can change. I’ll report outcomes Friday.
