Hospitality · Downtown Austin

Hotel Trinity opens on East Fifth with 258 rooms next to Antone’s

Austin American-Statesman · Community Impact · Nov 6, 2026

Weekend hospitality news: the Statesman reports Hotel Trinity welcomed its first guests Thursday at 311 E. Fifth St. — a 14-story, 258-room Marriott Autograph Collection property (20 suites, rooftop pool, nearly 6,700 SF of event space) managed by White Lodging as its 13th Austin hotel and the first downtown Autograph Collection flag here. Community Impact covers the Nov. 5 ribbon cutting with the Downtown Austin Alliance and Austin Chamber; DAA’s Davon Barbour said it’s the third downtown hotel opening this year and brings the downtown room count to about 16,000. Public dining is at ground-floor The Yacht Club; guests-only Sundeck Social will host Antone’s-curated “Sundeck Sessions.” My read: fresh rooms landing while the convention center rebuild runs toward a 2029 reopen — and after a year that already saw Hyatt Centric Congress Avenue and the Line Austin sold to lenders at foreclosure auction, per earlier Statesman reporting.

Data centers · State policy

The Real Deal: post-election data-center politics put Texas pipeline in the spotlight

The Real Deal · Nov 6, 2026 · Texas Tribune · Nov 4, 2026

Fresh national CRE framing from TRD: Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa’s campaign pledge to restrict new data centers is part of a broader election-night push for transparency, fewer tax breaks, and tighter water/energy rules — with Texas called out as having edged Virginia as the top market by under-construction capacity. The Texas Tribune’s post-election explainer remains the local policy backdrop: Hinojosa has called for a moratorium lasting until the Legislature meets in January to pass stronger protections. On the ground here, I’m still watching Hutto’s Dec. 1 code rewrite, Austin’s Dec. 3 Phase 1 data-center rules vote, and whether TCEQ’s STEERS mid-November target holds.

Capital markets · Statewide distressed CRE

CRE Daily: Texas foreclosure flags drop below $600M for November auctions

CRE Daily · The Real Deal · Nov 6, 2026

CRE Daily (also syndicated via Yahoo Finance), citing Roddy’s Foreclosure Listing Service via TRD, says flagged troubled Texas commercial loans for November auctions fell below $600 million after topping $1 billion in October — 33 properties on the list, with Harris County leading at 13. Apartments fill much of the new slate; Austin’s 70-unit Velo Flats is listed with an $11.3 million Thrive Lending loan from 2022. Nine loans are carryovers from earlier postings. Lower volume isn’t a clean bill of health — repeat flags often mean ongoing lender talks — but it’s a useful statewide stress check for MSA investors watching 2021–22 vintage multifamily debt.

City Hall · Follow-up

Still digesting Thursday: North Lamar DB90 and $695M AE bonds approved; Davis Lane to Nov. 19

Y’all City Hall · City of Austin Action Notes · Nov 5, 2026

Carrying forward from Thursday’s Council: North Lamar at Beaver Street neighborhood-plan and rezoning (Items 31–32) cleared on all three readings for the DB90 case staff framed around roughly 545 apartments and a small retail plaza; consent also authorized up to $695 million in Austin Energy revenue bonds (issue by April 22, 2027) and directed a targeted commercial-space rent buy-down program for eligible small businesses. The 3601 Davis Lane mini-warehouse rezoning was postponed to Nov. 19 at the applicant’s request.

Industrial · Semiconductor corridor

Taylor corridor check: reports still say AMD books most of Samsung Fab 2’s planned 2nm HBM base-die output

TweakTown · Nov 5, 2026 · Maeil Business Newspaper via TweakTown

No fresh groundbreaking date overnight. Trade coverage still has Samsung’s planned Taylor Fab 2 (~2.7M SF) with customers for its entire planned 2nm HBM base-die output before construction begins, AMD accounting for most of the volume (NVIDIA also mentioned), site prep underway, formal construction framed for this month, equipment move-in 2029, and mass production 2030. Samsung has not confirmed product mix — treat the customer story as reported. For CRE, a booked second fab still points to supplier, housing, and industrial demand along the Taylor corridor.
